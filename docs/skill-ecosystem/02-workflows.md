# 02 — Cross-kit workflow catalogue

The machine-readable source is [workflows.json](workflows.json). The six workflows below are reference paths, not mandatory recipes. Skip stages that do not serve the user's goal.

## 1. Idea to product delivery

**Trigger:** an open challenge or selected idea may become a product change.

Discovery evidence and supplied feedback can inform the brief. Creative exploration opens alternatives; concept development makes a selected direction discussable; evaluation exposes uncertainty; a small experiment may reduce a critical risk. Only after a direction is approved should prioritisation and product specification begin. Pending Specifications & Delivery skills can then convert requirements into stories, work breakdown and test evidence.

**Human gates:** choose the concept; approve experiment scope and participant contact; approve opportunity priority; approve product scope; approve release.

**Do not:** turn an interesting idea directly into a PRD, treat concept-test reactions as market validation, or let a scorecard make the decision.

## 2. Brand from discovery to launch

**Trigger:** a brand needs evidence-led strategy, identity and launch planning.

Audience and stakeholder research inform discovery. Brand strategy and positioning define the intended direction; identity methods test coherence. Campaign concepts and content plans translate approved strategy into expression. Quality and claim checks protect consistency and evidence.

**Human gates:** approve research scope, strategy, positioning, identity, claims and launch.

**Availability:** depends on the integration of PR #3.

## 3. Content to publishable media

**Trigger:** a content idea needs a repeatable path from brief to reviewed assets.

Plan the content, explore the angle if needed, design the production pipeline, then apply approved editing/colour decisions. Review outputs, create platform assets and obtain publication approval.

**Human gates:** approve creative direction, source assets, render settings and publication.

**Availability:** media-editing and grading stages depend on PR #2. The content planning and packaging skills are already available.

## 4. Operational improvement to automation

**Trigger:** an existing process has a measurable problem and automation may be one intervention.

Map the process and diagnose bottlenecks first. Test a process improvement where appropriate. Assess whether automation is justified; design the technology-agnostic workflow before platform-specific implementation. Validate integrations, mappings, failure paths and acceptance conditions.

**Human gates:** approve process changes, data access, integrations, production deployment and rollback plan.

**Availability:** workflow-process design depends on PR #1.

## 5. Agent from idea to governed release

**Trigger:** a new or materially changed AI agent is being designed.

Define the agent's purpose and boundaries, review least-privilege permissions, define approval gates, plan evaluations, prepare datasets and rubrics, run regression comparisons and review release readiness. Incidents feed back into the charter, controls and tests.

**Human gates:** approve permissions, consequential actions, evaluation thresholds and release.

**Important:** evaluation plans and datasets do not prove quality until tests are run and outputs reviewed.

## 6. Visual reference to finished look

**Trigger:** a visual direction needs to become a repeatable media treatment.

Research references, translate them into art direction, develop a repeatable look, apply grading and review the rendered output. Keep technical correction separate from subjective styling.

**Human gates:** approve references, look, source media, render and final output.

**Availability:** reference and art-direction stages depend on PR #3; media grading stages depend on PR #2.

## Workflow completion

A workflow is complete only when its stated outcome is reached or the user explicitly chooses to stop, park or return to an earlier stage. A generated handoff brief is not proof that the receiving skill ran. A proposed action is not an approved action.
