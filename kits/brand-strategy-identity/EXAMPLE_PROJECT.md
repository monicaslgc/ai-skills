# Fictional example: Northstar Studio

Entirely fictional example.

Northstar Studio is a fictional small business offering guided workshops for early-career designers. It operates in Portugal and plans to test an English-language offer in another European market. No customer research, sales data or competitor evidence is supplied.

## Expected workflow
1. Ask discovery questions about the offer, buyer, price model, proof, capacity and business goal.
2. Mark the proposed audience as unvalidated.
3. Research local alternatives and adjacent learning categories with dated sources.
4. Develop positioning options with explicit assumptions.
5. Generate naming directions only after the naming brief is approved; do not state availability.
6. Create visual direction and sample messaging labelled as drafts.
7. Request human approval before final name, identity, claims or launch.

The agent must not invent workshop outcomes, testimonials, market size, customer preferences or trademark availability.
