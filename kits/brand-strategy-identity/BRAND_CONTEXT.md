## Working rules
1. Label information as **fact**, **interpretation**, **hypothesis**, or **recommendation**.
2. Never convert a hypothesis into a fact when handing work to another skill.
3. If inputs conflict, preserve both sources, identify the conflict and request an owner decision.
4. If a field is unknown, leave it unknown; do not silently invent a value.
5. Do not overwrite approved content. Propose a change and record its impact.
6. Keep personal, confidential and commercially sensitive information out of public examples and external tools unless explicitly authorised.
