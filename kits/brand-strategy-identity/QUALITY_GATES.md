# Brand Skill Quality Gates

Apply these checks before handing work to another skill or presenting a deliverable as ready.

## Gate 1 — Input integrity
- Objective and intended decision are explicit.
- Relevant project context has been read.
- Missing inputs and assumptions are visible.
- Source material is dated and attributable where possible.

## Gate 2 — Domain quality
- The deliverable uses the method and criteria specific to its skill.
- Alternatives and trade-offs are considered where the task requires them.
- Recommendations are actionable and proportionate to the evidence.
- The skill does not claim to have performed unavailable research or tool actions.

## Gate 3 — Consistency
- The output does not contradict Approved or Locked decisions.
- Any conflict is surfaced with references to the affected decisions.
- Terms, audience definitions and claims match the shared Brand Context.
- A skill does not silently rewrite another skill's deliverable.

## Gate 4 — Safety and approval
- Legal, trademark, privacy, accessibility and substantiation risks are flagged where relevant.
- Human approval is requested for strategic, legal-sensitive and external-release decisions.
- Tool side effects are disclosed and gated by explicit authorisation.

## Gate 5 — Handoff
Every handoff includes:
1. Status and deliverable link / location.
2. Summary of the work completed.
3. Evidence and sources.
4. Assumptions and unresolved questions.
5. Decisions made or required, with decision IDs.
6. Risks, limitations and confidence.
7. Recommended next skill / action.
8. Human approval required (yes / no, and by whom).

A deliverable that fails a critical gate must be marked **Needs revision**, not passed downstream as complete.
