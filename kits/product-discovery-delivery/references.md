# Product Discovery & Delivery references

Use supplied research and approved product data as evidence. Distinguish direct user quotes, observed behaviour, interpretation and hypotheses. Avoid leading questions; do not generalise from unrepresentative samples. Make prioritisation criteria and weights visible. Requirements should include scope, non-goals, dependencies, telemetry and testable acceptance criteria. Release readiness requires evidence for support, privacy/security, analytics, rollout and rollback; unresolved critical checks remain blockers.
