# AI Quality & Evaluation references

Define intended behaviour before scoring. Use synthetic or approved examples; document provenance, labels and coverage. Separate development and holdout data. Rubrics need observable anchors and examples. Validate structure and business semantics separately. Track prompt, model, settings and dataset versions. Report sample size, uncertainty and known gaps; do not claim statistical significance without suitable design. Treat automated judges as fallible and use human review for consequential outputs.
