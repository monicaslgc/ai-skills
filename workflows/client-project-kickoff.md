# New client project, from scope to first status report

Start a client project with an agreed scope, a charter, a risk log and a rhythm of reporting.

**Use it when:** A client project has been sold and needs to start with clear responsibilities and reporting.

## Steps

| # | Skill | Input | Output | Gate (a person decides) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | [`statement-of-work-drafter`](../skills/statement-of-work-drafter) | Agreed requirements and quote | Scope document | Sales, legal and the client agree it |
| 2 | [`project-charter-writer`](../skills/project-charter-writer) | Scope document | Charter with goals, sponsor, team and constraints | Sponsor signs |
| 3 | [`project-plan-builder`](../skills/project-plan-builder) | Charter | Plan with phases, milestones and dependencies | Project owner approves |
| 4 | [`raid-log-manager`](../skills/raid-log-manager) | Plan and scope | Initial risks, assumptions, issues and dependencies with owners | Every item has an owner |
| 5 | [`meeting-minutes-and-action-log`](../skills/meeting-minutes-and-action-log) | Kickoff meeting notes | Minutes, decisions and actions | Attendees correct the minutes |
| 6 | [`status-and-performance-report`](../skills/status-and-performance-report) | Plan, log and actions | First status report | Project owner approves what is sent |

## Rules for this workflow

- The scope document and the charter agree on deliverables and dates.
- Every risk and action has an owner and a relative deadline.
- Reports compare like with like from the first one.
- Carry the open items and assumptions from each step into the next.
- Keep the `INTERNAL` line out of anything that goes outside.

## Skills to install

`statement-of-work-drafter`, `project-charter-writer`, `project-plan-builder`, `raid-log-manager`, `meeting-minutes-and-action-log`, `status-and-performance-report`

## Start prompt

> A new client project starts on <date>. Draft the scope document from these notes and list the open points.

All examples are invented. Do not paste real personal data, credentials or confidential contract text into a chat you do not control.
