# Brand strategy and identity workflow

| Stage | Inputs | Work | Exit condition |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Discovery | Brief and source material | Workshop, interviews, audit, audience research | Evidence log and open questions reviewed |
| 2. Market context | Category and geography | Category and competitor research | Category map and limitations documented |
| 3. Strategy | Discovery and market evidence | Positioning, personality, identity prism, architecture | Stakeholder-approved strategy |
| 4. Naming | Approved strategy and naming brief | Generate a raw batch; immediately run domain/company/trademark viability gate; develop concepts only for viable candidates; then run linguistic/cultural validation | Candidate passes mandatory constraints; final name selected by authorised decision-maker; legal clearance separate |
| 5. Art direction | Approved strategy | Visual references, moodboards and design brief | Direction approved before design production |
| 6. Expression | Approved identity direction | Messaging, voice, touchpoint applications, guidelines | Guidelines and assets reviewed |
| 7. Activation | Approved system | Campaigns, content, launch and localization | Launch approvals and owners confirmed |
| 8. Governance | Live brand system | Asset governance, audits and measurement | Review cadence and ownership established |

The order is a default, not a rigid waterfall. Revisit earlier decisions when evidence changes. Each stage returns status (draft / needs-input / ready-for-review / approved / blocked), summary, evidence, assumptions, decisions, open questions, risks, next skill and approval requirement. A complete-looking document is not approval.
