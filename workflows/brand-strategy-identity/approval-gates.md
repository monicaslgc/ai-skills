# Human approval gates

| Gate | Required review | Evidence | Agent may proceed? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Discovery baseline | Project sponsor / brand owner | Scope, audience, constraints and evidence gaps | Only after scope accepted |
| Brand strategy | Authorised business decision-maker | Positioning options, trade-offs, proof and risks | No, until explicit approval |
| Name shortlist | Brand owner | Rationale, linguistic screen, preliminary checks | Only to validation; not public use |
| Final name | Legal counsel and authorised owner | Jurisdiction-specific trademark advice and business decision | No, until clearance/approval |
| Visual direction | Brand owner | Annotated moodboards and strategic fit | No, before design production |
| Final identity | Brand owner and relevant reviewers | Assets, usage tests, licences and accessibility | No, before publication |
| Claims and campaign | Legal/compliance and marketing owner | Claim substantiation and channel context | No, before external release |
| Launch | Launch owner | Readiness, approved assets and correction plan | No, before publication |

Approval must be explicit, attributable and timestamped. Silence is not approval. Record the exact approved version. Publishing, spending, deleting, overwriting or disclosing information requires separate confirmation immediately before execution.
