# Job application, from posting to first 90 days

Apply with truthful, tailored documents and prepare to start well.

**Use it when:** You found a posting and want a tailored CV, cover letter, stories and a plan.

## Steps

| # | Skill | Input | Output | Gate (a person decides) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | [`job-posting-scorer`](../skills/job-posting-scorer) | Posting and your master CV | Fit score with gaps and honest framing | You decide whether to apply |
| 2 | [`cv-tailoring-from-master`](../skills/cv-tailoring-from-master) | Master CV, posting | Tailored CV using only confirmed facts | You check every claim |
| 3 | [`confidentiality-safe-wording`](../skills/confidentiality-safe-wording) | Achievements involving confidential work | Public wording without names or secrets | You approve the wording |
| 4 | [`cover-letter-writer`](../skills/cover-letter-writer) | Tailored CV, posting | Cover letter in the posting's language | You edit and approve |
| 5 | [`achievement-story-builder`](../skills/achievement-story-builder) | Your experience | Interview stories with situation, action and result | You rehearse them |
| 6 | [`new-role-90-day-plan`](../skills/new-role-90-day-plan) | Offer or final interview stage | First 30, 60 and 90 days plan with questions for the interviewer | You share it only if you choose |

## Rules for this workflow

- Only true statements go into any document.
- Never reuse confidential material from a former employer.
- Nothing is sent by the workflow; you send it.
- Carry the open items and assumptions from each step into the next.
- Keep the `INTERNAL` line out of anything that goes outside.

## Skills to install

`job-posting-scorer`, `cv-tailoring-from-master`, `confidentiality-safe-wording`, `cover-letter-writer`, `achievement-story-builder`, `new-role-90-day-plan`

## Start prompt

> Here is a job posting and my master CV. Score the fit first and tell me what to be careful about.

All examples are invented. Do not paste real personal data, credentials or confidential contract text into a chat you do not control.
