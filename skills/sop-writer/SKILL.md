# Sop writer

Capture how work is done so someone else can do it correctly without asking.

## Inputs to collect

Ask for what is missing, or mark it as unknown:
- The task and why it matters.
- Who does it, how often, with which tools.
- The people who know how it works.
- Known problems and exceptions.

## Workflow

1. State the purpose, scope (what is and is not covered), and the roles involved.
2. List prerequisites: access, tools, inputs and anything to check before starting.
3. Write numbered steps, each starting with a verb and describing one action. Add the expected result or a check after important steps.
4. Add warnings where mistakes are costly, and what to do if a step fails.
5. Add exceptions and escalation: when to stop and who to contact.
6. Define records: what must be saved or logged, and where.
7. Add version, owner, review date and change history.
8. Test the SOP by having someone unfamiliar follow it, and fix unclear steps.

## Output format

```
SOP: <title>   Version / Owner / Review date
Purpose / Scope / Roles
Prerequisites
Steps (1..n) with checks
Exceptions and escalation
Records
Change history
```

## Safety

- Remove credentials, personal data and internal-only detail from shared copies.
- Mark steps with safety or legal impact clearly.
