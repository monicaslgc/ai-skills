# User story: multi-unit-performance-comparator

## Audience
An operations manager or business lead who needs to produce a fair, transparent comparison of multiple business units without mistaking raw differences for performance differences.

## Scenario
The user supplies operational context that may be incomplete and needs a repeatable artefact that can be reviewed before action.

## Acceptance criteria
- Metrics are comparable or explicitly marked as not comparable.
- Raw values and formulas remain traceable.
- Differences are not presented as causal explanations.
- No invented benchmarks or adjustment factors.
- Consequential decisions remain subject to accountable human approval.

## Try it
> Compare these units fairly. Check metric comparability, account for supplied differences in size and maturity, show formulas and caveats, and identify patterns for investigation rather than declaring winners.
