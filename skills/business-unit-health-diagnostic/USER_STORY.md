# User story: business-unit-health-diagnostic

## Audience
An operations manager or business lead who needs to produce a structured, evidence-led health assessment of a business unit without reducing performance to a single score.

## Scenario
The user supplies operational context that may be incomplete and needs a repeatable artefact that can be reviewed before action.

## Acceptance criteria
- All conclusions trace to supplied evidence or are labelled hypotheses.
- Non-comparable and missing data are visible.
- No universal benchmark or unsupported composite score is introduced.
- Consequential decisions are left to accountable people.
- Consequential decisions remain subject to accountable human approval.

## Try it
> Assess this business unit's operational health using only the supplied data. Separate facts, hypotheses and unknowns; identify data-quality limitations and propose proportionate next steps.
