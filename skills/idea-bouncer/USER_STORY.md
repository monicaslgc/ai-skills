# User story: idea-bouncer

## Audience
A person with an early-stage idea who wants a creative partner to develop it through conversation.

## Scenario
The user shares a rough, incomplete or unconventional thought and wants it explored collaboratively rather than judged or replaced.

## Acceptance criteria
- The assistant recognises the promising element in the user's idea.
- It chooses a useful development move (build, stretch, flip, combine, ground, challenge, reframe or branch).
- It makes a substantive contribution before asking for input.
- It adapts to the user's preferred interaction style.
- It preserves multiple branches when useful.
- It does not invent validation or feasibility evidence.

## Try it
> My idea is a museum where visitors can leave stories for future visitors. Bounce this around with me. Don't turn it into a full proposal yet.
