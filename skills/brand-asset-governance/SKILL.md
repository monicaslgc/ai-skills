# Brand Asset Governance

## Purpose
Design governance for brand files and reusable assets.

## Required inputs
- User objective and required decision or deliverable.
- Relevant organisation, product, audience, market and language context.
- Existing source material, constraints and approved decisions.
- Unknowns; ask only for information that materially changes the work.

## Method
Define source of truth, naming, metadata, permissions, versioning, owners, approval states, expiry and archival; specify request and retirement workflows.

## Output
1. Outcome: requested artefact or recommendation.
2. Evidence: source, date and confidence for material factual claims.
3. Assumptions and unknowns, separate from verified facts.
4. Decisions needed, owner and approval required.
5. Smallest useful next step.

## Quality and safety
- Preserve supplied source facts; do not silently fill gaps.
- Separate evidence, interpretation, hypothesis and recommendation.
- Prefer primary sources and record URLs and access dates.
- Protect confidential and personal information; use synthetic examples in public artefacts.
- Never overwrite approved master assets without versioning and authorised approval.
- Follow the shared contracts in ../../kits/brand-strategy-identity/README.md when tools are available. If a tool is unavailable, state the limitation and provide a manual alternative.

## Decision rules
- Define canonical storage, asset taxonomy, metadata, naming convention, file formats, owners, access roles and retention policy.
- Specify lifecycle states (working, in review, approved, published, deprecated, archived) and what actions each role may perform.
- Require version history, change rationale, approval evidence and rollback path for master assets.
- Define request, review, release, localisation, expiry and retirement workflows, including service-level expectations where known.
- Separate source files, exports, templates and licensed third-party assets; track licence scope and expiry.
- Test governance with a new campaign request, urgent correction, staff departure and asset retirement scenario.
- Never overwrite or delete the canonical approved asset without authorised approval and recoverability.
