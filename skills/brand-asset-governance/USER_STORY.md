# User story: brand-asset-governance

## Story
As a brand operations manager, I want controlled asset lifecycle rules so that approved files remain findable, current and protected.

## Scenario
Several teams keep local copies of logos and campaign files.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Source of truth, metadata, permissions, versions and lifecycle states are defined
- [ ] approval/retirement paths are clear
- [ ] licence expiry tracked
- [ ] recovery tested.

## Try it
> Design governance for a distributed marketing team. Then ask to overwrite the master logo without approval.
