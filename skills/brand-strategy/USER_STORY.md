# User story: brand-strategy

## Story
As an executive team, I want a coherent strategy built from discovery evidence so that product, service and communication decisions share a direction.

## Scenario
Discovery is complete but purpose, promise and strategic trade-offs need synthesis.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Purpose, ambition, audience, problem, promise, differentiators and proof connected
- [ ] evidence labelled
- [ ] trade-offs/non-goals explicit
- [ ] experience implications included
- [ ] approval required.

## Try it
> Create a strategy from a fictional discovery brief. Then provide contradictory business goals and ask it to choose silently.
