# User story: status-report-writer

## Story

**As** a project manager reporting to a steering group,
**I want** a short report that leads with an honest status and the decisions I need,
**so that** stakeholders understand the situation in two minutes and act on what is asked of them.

## Scenario

The project slipped two weeks because a vendor delivered late, and I need a decision on extra budget.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The first line states the status and the reason.
- [ ] Progress is stated in facts, not adjectives.
- [ ] Decisions needed are listed with an owner and date.
- [ ] Risks have a response and owner.
- [ ] The report fits on one page.
