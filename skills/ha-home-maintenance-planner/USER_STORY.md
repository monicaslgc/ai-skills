# User story: ha-home-maintenance-planner

## Story

**As** a homeowner who forgets maintenance,
**I want** a maintenance schedule with reminders,
**so that** the home stays in good order without mental effort.

## Scenario

I keep forgetting to replace filters and test smoke detectors.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Tasks and intervals are proposed and confirmed.
- [ ] Manufacturer guidance takes priority.
- [ ] Reminder methods are checked before use.
- [ ] Reminders are created only after approval.
- [ ] Hazardous repairs are not described.
