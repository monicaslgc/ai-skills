# User story: customer-playbook-builder

## Story

**As** someone who handles the same customer situation again and again,
**I want** the best way of handling it written as a playbook, with the safe parts automated,
**so that** every customer gets the same good handling and new colleagues ramp up faster.

## Scenario

Every time a customer's usage drops after go-live, each manager reacts differently, and the useful steps live in one person's head.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A trigger, exclusions, a goal and a success measure.
- [ ] Numbered steps with owners, times and decision points taken from real cases.
- [ ] Message templates and an escalation rule.
- [ ] Automation candidates with safeguards and human approval where needed.
- [ ] Measures, an owner and a review date.
