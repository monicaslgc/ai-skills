# Creative Perspective Lenses

## Purpose
Expose dimensions of a challenge that one default viewpoint may miss. This is a reusable capability for Creative Room sessions and ordinary collaboration.

## Lens library
Select only lenses that add a different kind of thinking:
- **Visionary — possibility:** What could exist if current assumptions were relaxed?
- **Strategist — purpose and positioning:** What matters, for whom, and why would this direction make sense?
- **Artist — meaning and experience:** What should people feel, notice, remember or interpret?
- **Inventor — mechanism:** What new interaction, material, system or sequence could make it work?
- **Challenger — critical inquiry:** Which assumption, trade-off or neglected consequence deserves scrutiny?
- **Synthesizer — patterns:** What ideas, tensions or fragments connect into a more interesting whole?

These are temporary reasoning lenses, not characters, credentials, real people or independent agents.

## Procedure
1. Restate the challenge in one sentence; preserve its intended outcome.
2. Choose 2–4 lenses based on what is missing. Use all six only when the user asks for a full room.
3. Give each lens a distinct question and contribution. Do not merely rewrite one idea in six voices.
4. Make contributions concrete: a possibility, mechanism, experience, risk, reframing or connection.
5. Label assumptions and speculation. Do not invent evidence or claim external validation.
6. Return control to the user: invite a reaction, rejection, combination or new lens.

## Quality rules
- Prefer complementary perspectives over equal airtime.
- Avoid stereotypes and theatrical dialogue.
- Do not force consensus.
- The Challenger tests ideas; it does not act as a generic pessimist.
- The Synthesizer connects after material exists; it must not close exploration too early.
- Preserve the user's language and original contributions.

## Output shape
Use a compact round:
- **Lens / question**
- **Contribution**
- **What it opens up**
- **Uncertainty or tension** (when relevant)

## Acceptance checks
- Each selected lens contributes a different insight.
- Contributions remain anchored to the challenge.
- Speculation is distinguishable from evidence.
- No fictional people or separate-agent execution is implied.
- The user has a clear next move.
