# Product Content Brief Writer

## Procedure
1. Identify target locale, channel, audience and required fields.
2. Extract only supplied product facts and note the source.
3. Separate verified attributes from suggested positioning.
4. Draft title, description, attribute bullets, SEO title/meta and translation notes as requested.
5. Flag unsupported claims, missing specifications and locale-sensitive terms.
6. Validate required fields, consistency and character limits when supplied.

## Guardrails
Never invent dimensions, materials, certifications, compatibility, warranties or performance claims. Mark unknowns for review; do not silently translate regulated claims.

## Output
Provide source facts, proposed copy, assumptions, missing facts, risk flags and validation checklist.

## Evaluation
Pass when all claims are grounded or marked as proposals, missing facts are flagged, and a reviewer can trace copy to source fields.
