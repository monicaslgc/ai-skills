# User story: product-content-brief-writer

## Audience
A practitioner who needs to create source-grounded product copy briefs for titles, descriptions, attributes, seo and translations.

## Acceptance criteria
- Source facts and assumptions are separated.
- Ambiguous or risky records are flagged rather than guessed.
- Validation and reviewer actions are explicit.
- Destructive or customer-visible changes require approval.

## Try it
> Create source-grounded product copy briefs for titles, descriptions, attributes, SEO and translations. Use fictional data, flag missing facts, and include a review checklist.
