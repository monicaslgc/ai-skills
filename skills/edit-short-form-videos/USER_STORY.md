# User story: edit-short-form-videos

## Story

**As** a creator editing gameplay into short vertical videos,
**I want** an edit plan, reframing and repairs based on the real footage,
**so that** my videos are accurate, clean and ready to publish after my review.

## Scenario

A clip has a white rectangle in the opening seconds and needs reframing to 9:16 with subtitles.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Timestamps come from inspected footage, and source time is distinguished from timeline time.
- [ ] The first frame and the exact defect period are checked after a repair.
- [ ] Duration, dimensions and audio are preserved unless asked otherwise.
- [ ] Originals are never changed; outputs are versioned.
- [ ] Nothing is published or scheduled without explicit permission.
