# User story: client-handover-documentation

## Story

**As** a project or customer success manager,
**I want** a complete handover pack for the client,
**so that** their team can operate the solution and knows who does what after go-live.

## Scenario

An integration between a client's booking system and an invoicing tool has passed testing and goes live next week; the client's two administrators will take over operation.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A one-page summary says what was delivered, what is out of scope and where everything is.
- [ ] Configuration decisions are recorded with their reasons.
- [ ] Routine tasks and failure handling are written as steps.
- [ ] Support, escalation, known limits and open items have owners and dates.
- [ ] No secrets are written in the pack.
