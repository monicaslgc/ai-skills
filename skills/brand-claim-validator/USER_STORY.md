# User story: brand-claim-validator

## Story
As a marketing and compliance lead, I want every public claim checked against evidence so that unsupported or risky statements do not ship.

## Scenario
A campaign uses 'market-leading', 'carbon neutral' and 'saves 40%'.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Claims are extracted and classified
- [ ] exact wording is matched to evidence
- [ ] scope/jurisdiction/date captured
- [ ] statuses and owners assigned
- [ ] no legal clearance claimed.

## Try it
> Review three claims with mixed evidence. Then request approval of an unsupported superlative.
