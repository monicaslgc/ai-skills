# Brand Claim Validator

## Purpose
Check brand and marketing claims for evidence and risk.

## Required inputs
- The user's objective and the decision or deliverable required.
- Relevant organisation, product, audience, market and language context.
- Existing source material, constraints and approved decisions.
- Unknowns; ask only for information that materially changes the work.

## Method
Extract factual, comparative, superlative, environmental and performance claims; map each to evidence, scope, date and owner; flag missing substantiation and legal review needs.

## Output
1. **Outcome:** the requested artefact or recommendation.
2. **Evidence:** source, date and confidence for material factual claims.
3. **Assumptions and unknowns:** separate from verified facts.
4. **Decisions needed:** owner and approval required, if any.
5. **Next step:** the smallest useful action.

## Quality and safety
- Preserve the user's terminology and supplied source facts; do not silently fill gaps.
- Separate evidence, interpretation, hypothesis and recommendation.
- Prefer primary sources and record URLs and access dates for research.
- Protect confidential, personal and commercially sensitive information; use synthetic examples in public artefacts.
- Do not provide legal clearance. Mark unsupported claims as blocked.
- Follow the shared contracts in ../../kits/brand-strategy-identity/README.md when tools are available. If a tool is unavailable, state the limitation and provide a manual alternative.

## Decision rules
- Extract each explicit and implied claim, then classify it (objective fact, comparative, superlative, performance, environmental, health/financial, testimonial or guarantee).
- Record exact wording, channel, audience, market/jurisdiction, evidence owner, source, date, methodology, scope and expiry.
- Check that evidence supports the precise wording and that qualifiers are visible and not contradicted elsewhere.
- Flag comparisons for like-for-like basis, named comparator, timeframe and reproducible methodology.
- Flag environmental and impact claims for scope, lifecycle boundary, substantiation and applicable jurisdictional review.
- Use statuses: supported, supported with qualification, needs evidence, blocked, legal review required.
- Never issue legal clearance. A missing or weak evidence base blocks publication until the accountable reviewer resolves it.
