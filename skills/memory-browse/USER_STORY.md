# User story: memory-browse

## Story

**As** someone with a large archive of saved conversations,
**I want** to see an overview of what is stored, grouped by project or date,
**so that** I can understand what I have and pick what to open.

## Scenario

I want to review everything I saved about one area over the last quarter.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The assistant lists recent conversations grouped by project or date.
- [ ] Filters by source and project are applied when I mention them.
- [ ] The list stays concise and offers to open any single item in full.
