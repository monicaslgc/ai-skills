# User story: creative-session

## Audience
Anyone who wants to think through an open-ended challenge with an AI partner or a simulated room of different thinking perspectives.

## Scenario
The user has an idea, problem or opportunity but does not yet know the best direction. They want an interactive process rather than a one-shot list of suggestions.

## Acceptance criteria
- The session can operate in Creative Partner and Creative Room modes.
- The facilitator frames the challenge without asking unnecessary questions.
- Specialist ideation skills are used when relevant.
- Divergence and convergence are deliberately separated.
- The user can redirect, pause, reject or combine ideas at any time.
- Facts, assumptions, hypotheses and decisions are distinguishable.
- A reusable session record can be produced on request.

## Try it
> Run a Creative Room session to explore a new way for a local community to share skills. Start with divergent thinking, use at least three distinct perspectives, and do not rank ideas until I ask.
