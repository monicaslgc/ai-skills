# User story: brand-audit

## Story
**As** a business or marketing lead, **I want** an evidence-led assessment of the whole brand system, **so that** I can prioritise strategic and execution improvements rather than jumping straight to a visual refresh.

## Scenario
An established organisation suspects its brand no longer reflects its offer and wants to understand the causes before investing in a rebrand.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Scope, markets, touchpoints, audience and intended decision are defined.
- [ ] The audit assesses strategy, audience relevance, distinctiveness, messaging, identity, experience and governance.
- [ ] Findings cite observable evidence and include severity, confidence and impact.
- [ ] Systemic strategic issues are distinguished from isolated execution errors.
- [ ] Performance claims are not inferred from visual inspection.
- [ ] Recommendations are prioritised by impact, effort, dependencies and reversibility.
- [ ] The output distinguishes quick fixes from decisions requiring leadership approval.

## Try it
> Audit a fictional regional hotel brand whose website, social channels and in-person experience appear inconsistent. Identify what evidence would be needed before recommending a rebrand.
