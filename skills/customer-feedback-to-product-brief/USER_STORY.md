# User story: customer-feedback-to-product-brief

## Story

**As** the person closest to a group of customers,
**I want** to hand Product and Engineering well-evidenced feedback,
**so that** the right problems are fixed and customers hear what happened to what they told us.

## Scenario

Six customers asked in different ways for better export options, and two reported a bug in the same screen. The feedback sits in tickets and call notes.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Feedback is separated into bugs, requests and usability issues.
- [ ] The customer's goal behind each request is written, not only their proposed solution.
- [ ] Themes carry counts of customers and reports.
- [ ] Impact, workaround and evidence are included; bugs have reproduction steps.
- [ ] A priority proposal with reasons and unknowns.
- [ ] A plan to tell each customer the outcome.

## Try it

> Turn these tickets and call notes about exports into a brief for Product, with counts, impact and evidence, and a message back to the customers.
