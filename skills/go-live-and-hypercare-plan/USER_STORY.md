# User story: go-live-and-hypercare-plan

## Story

**As** the person who takes a customer live,
**I want** a go-live plan with a rollback and a watched hypercare period,
**so that** problems are caught early and the customer is never left alone after launch.

## Scenario

A customer goes live on Thursday with a new integration. Two defects are still open and the technical contact is on holiday next week.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Go-live criteria are listed with status and evidence; waivers are named.
- [ ] Cutover steps and a rollback with trigger, owner and duration exist.
- [ ] Communication before, during and after is planned.
- [ ] Hypercare has duration, channel, contacts and daily watch thresholds.
- [ ] Exit criteria and the handover to normal support are defined.
