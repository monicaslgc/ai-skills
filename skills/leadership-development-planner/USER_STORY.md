# User story: leadership-development-planner

## Audience
An operations manager or business lead who needs to create a realistic development plan for a manager based on role expectations, observed work and agreed goals.

## Scenario
The user supplies operational context that may be incomplete and needs a repeatable artefact that can be reviewed before action.

## Acceptance criteria
- Goals are role-relevant and behaviourally observable.
- Evidence is distinguished from opinion.
- The plan is feasible and includes organisational support.
- Sensitive or consequential HR decisions remain with authorised humans.
- Consequential decisions remain subject to accountable human approval.

## Try it
> Draft a leadership development plan from these role expectations and observed examples. Separate evidence from interpretation, propose practical learning activities and define observable progress checks.
