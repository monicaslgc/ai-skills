# Brand Architecture

## Purpose
Recommend a brand architecture for an organisation or portfolio.

## Required inputs
- The user's objective and the decision or deliverable required.
- Relevant organisation, product, audience, market and language context.
- Existing source material, constraints and approved decisions.
- Unknowns; ask only for information that materially changes the work.

## Method
Inventory brands, products, audiences and relationships; compare branded house, endorsed, sub-brand and house-of-brands options; assess clarity, equity, cost and governance.

## Output
1. **Outcome:** the requested artefact or recommendation.
2. **Evidence:** source, date and confidence for material factual claims.
3. **Assumptions and unknowns:** separate from verified facts.
4. **Decisions needed:** owner and approval required, if any.
5. **Next step:** the smallest useful action.

## Quality and safety
- Preserve the user's terminology and supplied source facts; do not silently fill gaps.
- Separate evidence, interpretation, hypothesis and recommendation.
- Prefer primary sources and record URLs and access dates for research.
- Protect confidential, personal and commercially sensitive information; use synthetic examples in public artefacts.
- Do not rename or merge entities without stakeholder approval and legal review.
- Follow the shared contracts in ../../kits/brand-strategy-identity/README.md when tools are available. If a tool is unavailable, state the limitation and provide a manual alternative.

## Decision rules
- Inventory parent organisation, business units, products, services, acquired brands, audiences, markets and existing equity.
- Compare branded house, endorsed brands, sub-brands and house of brands against clarity, trust transfer, flexibility, acquisition integration, cost and governance.
- Model at least one real customer journey to expose naming and navigation confusion.
- Evaluate migration complexity: legal entities, contracts, domains, packaging, systems, customer support and internal adoption.
- Recommend principles and decision criteria before drawing a hierarchy; show trade-offs and transition stages.
- Do not recommend renaming, merging or retiring a brand without owner approval and legal/regulatory review.
- Output a hierarchy diagram description, naming rules, ownership model, migration risks and decisions required.
