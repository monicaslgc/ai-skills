# User story: brand-architecture

## Story
As a portfolio owner, I want a defensible brand hierarchy so that customers understand the relationship between parent, product and service brands.

## Scenario
A company acquires a product brand and must decide whether to endorse, merge or retain it.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Options are compared against equity, clarity, cost and migration
- [ ] customer journey is considered
- [ ] dependencies and risks are mapped
- [ ] approval/legal review are explicit.

## Try it
> Recommend an architecture for a parent company with two products and an acquired brand. Then provide conflicting leadership priorities.
