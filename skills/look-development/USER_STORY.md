# User story: look-development

## Story
**As** a creator, **I want** a visual mood translated into repeatable settings, **so that** I can reuse a look without relying on vague instructions.

## Synthetic scenario
A fictional travel sequence needs a natural warm daylight look, not a heavy cinematic teal-orange grade.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] The brief becomes observable image characteristics.
- [ ] Technical correction is separate from creative styling.
- [ ] Parameters are bounded and tool-specific.
- [ ] A preview is required before batch rendering.
- [ ] Preset version and rollback instructions are included.

## Try it
“Translate ‘natural warm daylight’ into a restrained preset for SDR media, and define a preview acceptance checklist.”
