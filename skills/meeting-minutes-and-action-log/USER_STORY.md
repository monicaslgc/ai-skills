# User story: meeting-minutes-and-action-log

## Story

**As** someone who runs customer and project meetings,
**I want** minutes that capture decisions and actions accurately,
**so that** everyone knows what was agreed and nobody reopens settled points.

## Scenario

A steering call with a customer and a partner ends with three decisions, seven actions, two open questions and an action from the last call that is late.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Decisions are separated from discussion.
- [ ] Each action has an owner and a due date, or is marked to confirm.
- [ ] Previous actions are reviewed.
- [ ] A follow-up message and decision-log entries are produced.
- [ ] The external version leaves out internal remarks.
