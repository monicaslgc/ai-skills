# User story: new-role-90-day-plan

## Story

**As** someone about to start, or interview for, a new role,
**I want** a realistic first-90-days plan,
**so that** I learn the right things, deliver an early win and agree what success means with my manager.

## Scenario

A candidate is in the final interview stage for a customer-facing role in a new sector and is asked how they would approach their first three months.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The role's purpose and year-one results are written for the manager to confirm.
- [ ] Learn, contribute and own are planned in 30, 60 and 90 days with concrete outcomes and evidence.
- [ ] A relationship plan and two or three safe early wins.
- [ ] Check-ins and updates are scheduled.
- [ ] Risks and learning gaps are stated honestly.

## Try it

> Build a 90-day plan for this customer-facing role from the job description. I do not know the sector yet, so make the learning plan explicit.
