# User story: industry-domain-primer

## Story

**As** someone starting work in a sector I do not know,
**I want** a quick, sourced primer of how the sector works and what it calls things,
**so that** I can follow customers, ask good questions and be honest about what I still need to learn.

## Scenario

A new customer-facing role in a sector I have not worked in starts in three weeks, and the first customer calls are in the second week.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Players, money flow, value chain and core processes are mapped.
- [ ] Key objects, states and identifiers are listed.
- [ ] A glossary with examples that separates near-synonyms.
- [ ] Rules, seasons and typical problems are listed.
- [ ] Every important fact has a source, a date and a confidence level.
- [ ] Open questions for experts and a plan to close them.

## Try it

> Build a primer on how this sector works (who the players are, how money flows, the key terms and the typical problems) so I can hold a customer call in two weeks. Mark what is verified.
