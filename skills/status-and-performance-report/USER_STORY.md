# User story: status-and-performance-report

## Story

**As** a project or customer success manager,
**I want** a clear periodic report with checked figures,
**so that** the client or management sees where things stand and what needs a decision.

## Scenario

A monthly service report for a customer: ticket volumes, response-time targets, an integration project that slipped two weeks, and a pending decision about extra training.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The headline and overall status come first, with the reason.
- [ ] Each figure has a definition, a source, a target and a change since last period.
- [ ] Late items are explained with a new date.
- [ ] Risks and decisions have owners and dates.
- [ ] Gaps and estimates are marked, not hidden.
