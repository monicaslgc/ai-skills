# Brand Content Planner

## Purpose
Build a content system aligned with brand positioning.

## Required inputs
- User objective and required decision or deliverable.
- Relevant organisation, product, audience, market and language context.
- Existing source material, constraints and approved decisions.
- Unknowns; ask only for information that materially changes the work.

## Method
Define content pillars, audience needs, formats, cadence, channel roles, editorial workflow, accessibility and measurement; produce a realistic calendar and briefs.

## Output
1. Outcome: requested artefact or recommendation.
2. Evidence: source, date and confidence for material factual claims.
3. Assumptions and unknowns, separate from verified facts.
4. Decisions needed, owner and approval required.
5. Smallest useful next step.

## Quality and safety
- Preserve supplied source facts; do not silently fill gaps.
- Separate evidence, interpretation, hypothesis and recommendation.
- Prefer primary sources and record URLs and access dates.
- Protect confidential and personal information; use synthetic examples in public artefacts.
- Do not optimise for volume at the expense of strategic relevance or factual accuracy.
- Follow the shared contracts in ../../kits/brand-strategy-identity/README.md when tools are available. If a tool is unavailable, state the limitation and provide a manual alternative.

## Decision rules
- Map content pillars to approved positioning, audience needs, funnel/task and measurable purpose; reject topics with no strategic role.
- Assign each format a channel role, owner, production effort, review path, accessibility requirements and reuse potential.
- Build cadence from actual capacity and dependencies; do not assume daily publishing is desirable.
- Include a content brief with audience, insight/source, message, proof, format, CTA, distribution and success measure.
- Plan a realistic calendar with status, deadlines, approvers and dependencies; mark tentative dates as tentative.
- Include maintenance: review dates, factual refresh, localisation and retirement criteria.
- Do not promise reach, engagement or conversion outcomes; define hypotheses and measurement instead.
