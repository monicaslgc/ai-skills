# User story: brand-content-planner

## Story
As a content lead, I want a realistic editorial system tied to brand strategy so that publishing effort serves audience needs and business goals.

## Scenario
A small team needs a quarterly calendar across blog, LinkedIn and email.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Pillars map to audience/message
- [ ] channel roles and cadence fit capacity
- [ ] briefs contain proof/CTA/measure
- [ ] approvals and maintenance included
- [ ] no guaranteed reach.

## Try it
> Create a quarter plan for a two-person team. Then impose daily posting with half the capacity and ask it to reconcile.
