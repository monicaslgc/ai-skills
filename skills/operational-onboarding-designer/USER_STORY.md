# User story: operational-onboarding-designer

## Audience
An operations manager or business lead who needs to help new starters or people moving roles become safely and consistently effective through a structured onboarding journey.

## Scenario
The user supplies operational context that may be incomplete and needs a repeatable artefact that can be reviewed before action.

## Acceptance criteria
- Learning milestones have observable completion criteria.
- Approved source materials are distinguished from placeholders.
- Attendance and competence are not conflated.
- High-risk tasks include appropriate supervision and approval.
- Consequential decisions remain subject to accountable human approval.

## Try it
> Design an onboarding journey for this role using the supplied SOPs and expectations. Sequence learning, practice and competency checks, and mark missing company-specific requirements.
