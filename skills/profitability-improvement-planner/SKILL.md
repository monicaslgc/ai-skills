# Profitability Improvement Planner

## Purpose
Develop testable profitability improvement options without presenting speculative savings as guaranteed.

## Procedure
1. Confirm the unit economics, scope, period, currency, accounting definitions and objective.
2. Validate supplied revenue and cost data, including fixed versus variable costs, allocation rules and one-off items where known.
3. Identify evidenced drivers and possible opportunities such as price/mix, avoidable rework, utilisation or service cost; do not assume each applies.
4. For each option, state the mechanism, evidence needed, dependencies, trade-offs and who must validate it.
5. Calculate impact only where inputs and formulas support it; label scenarios and assumptions and avoid double-counting.
6. Include service quality, customer impact, workload and controls as counter-metrics where relevant.
7. Propose a validation step before committing resources or recognising benefits.

## Guardrails
Do not invent savings, margins, forecasts or causal claims. Distinguish revenue, gross margin, operating profit and cash impact. Financial decisions require validation by the accountable finance or business owner.

## Output contract
- Validated baseline and data caveats
- Potential improvement options
- Calculations or labelled scenarios
- Trade-offs, dependencies and counter-metrics
- Validation plan and approval requirements

## Acceptance checks
- Financial definitions and periods are explicit.
- Every estimate is reproducible or marked illustrative.
- Double-counting risks are addressed.
- No savings are presented as realised before verification.

## Example prompt
"Identify potential profitability improvements from these figures. Validate definitions, show reproducible calculations, label scenarios, include counter-metrics and propose tests before savings are claimed."

## Evaluation
Pass when claims are evidence-based, uncertainty is visible and approval boundaries are respected. Fail when facts, targets or outcomes are invented.
