# User story: profitability-improvement-planner

## Audience
An operations manager or business lead who needs to develop testable profitability improvement options without presenting speculative savings as guaranteed.

## Scenario
The user supplies context that may be incomplete and needs a repeatable artefact for review before action.

## Acceptance criteria
- Financial definitions and periods are explicit.
- Every estimate is reproducible or marked illustrative.
- Double-counting risks are addressed.
- No savings are presented as realised before verification.
- Consequential decisions remain subject to accountable human approval.

## Try it
> Identify potential profitability improvements from these figures. Validate definitions, show reproducible calculations, label scenarios, include counter-metrics and propose tests before savings are claimed.
