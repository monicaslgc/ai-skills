# User story: international-customer-communication

## Story

**As** a customer-facing manager with customers in several countries,
**I want** to communicate in a way people with different languages and calendars understand,
**so that** requests are clear, meetings work for everyone and nothing is missed in translation.

## Scenario

A customer team is split between two countries with a one-hour difference, English is their second language and a public holiday falls in the middle of the planned workshop week.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A working language and a channel for each message type.
- [ ] Messages are plain, lead with the point and state who does what by when.
- [ ] Dates carry a format and a time zone.
- [ ] Meetings are planned across time zones and holidays.
- [ ] Every meeting has a written summary with owners and dates.
