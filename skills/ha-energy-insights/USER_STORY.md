# User story: ha-energy-insights

## Story

**As** a homeowner worried about energy use,
**I want** an analysis of consumption with practical ideas,
**so that** I reduce waste and understand my bill.

## Scenario

My electricity bill rose and I do not know why.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Power and energy sensors are identified by unit.
- [ ] Totals, patterns, peaks and standby load are shown.
- [ ] Unmonitored loads are acknowledged.
- [ ] Savings are estimates with effort levels.
- [ ] Nothing is changed without confirmation.

## Try it

> Analyse my last week of energy use and tell me where I could save.
