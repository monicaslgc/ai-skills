# User story: diagram-with-brand-kit

## Story

**As** someone who publishes technical work under their own name or brand,
**I want** diagrams that look like they belong to the same family,
**so that** readers recognise the work and understand it at a glance.

## Scenario

A README, a website case study and a social post all need the same architecture diagram, in the brand's colours and fonts, and the first draft has labels overlapping the lines.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The title states one idea.
- [ ] No more than about ten nodes, each kind of node visibly different.
- [ ] The kit's colours, fonts, shapes and footer are used consistently.
- [ ] The image was rendered and inspected; no overlapping text or lines through labels.
- [ ] Invented or simulated parts are labelled.
- [ ] Source, image and alt text are delivered.

## Try it

> Draw a diagram of how our nightly sync, the invoice job and the approval step fit together. Use this colour and font kit, and give me the SVG, the PNG and the alt text.
