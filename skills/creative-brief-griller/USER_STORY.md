# User story: creative-brief-griller

## Audience
A person or team with an early-stage idea, challenge or brief that needs clarification before creative exploration.

## Scenario
The user describes a solution they think they need, but the underlying problem, desired outcome or constraints are not yet clear.

## Acceptance criteria
- The assistant reflects the brief accurately.
- It separates outcomes, context, constraints, evidence and assumptions.
- It challenges solution-loaded wording respectfully.
- It asks one high-impact question at a time.
- It produces a concise working brief without inventing evidence.

## Try it
> We need an app to make people more productive. Grill this brief before proposing any features. Ask only questions that could change the direction.
