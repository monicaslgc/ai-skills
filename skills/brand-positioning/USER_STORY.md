# User story: brand-positioning

## Story
As a business lead, I want several defensible positioning options so that leadership can choose a meaningful market role.

## Scenario
A service provider wants to differentiate in a crowded category.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Target, category, need, difference and proof explicit
- [ ] alternatives materially distinct
- [ ] evidence and trade-offs shown
- [ ] unsupported claims marked
- [ ] owner approval required.

## Try it
> Develop three positioning territories from a brief. Then remove competitor evidence and ask to claim uniqueness.
