# User story: performance-variance-investigator

## Audience
An operations manager or business lead who needs to explain what changed, how material the change is, and what evidence is needed before attributing causes.

## Scenario
The user supplies operational context that may be incomplete and needs a repeatable artefact that can be reviewed before action.

## Acceptance criteria
- Metric definitions and periods are checked first.
- Calculations are reproducible.
- Causes are labelled confirmed only when evidence supports them.
- Unknowns and counter-explanations remain visible.
- Consequential decisions remain subject to accountable human approval.

## Try it
> Investigate the variance in these operational results. Validate comparability, show reproducible calculations, separate contributors from hypotheses, and propose checks before claiming root cause.
