# User story: brand-voice-copywriter

## Story
**As** a content designer or marketing lead, **I want** practical voice rules and examples, **so that** different teams can write consistently without making every channel sound identical.

## Scenario
A company has agreed its positioning and key messages but its website, support team and product UI sound like different organisations.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] The voice guide is grounded in approved positioning and messaging, or labelled provisional.
- [ ] It defines 3–5 observable voice principles with examples and anti-examples.
- [ ] Tone variations are described for relevant contexts without changing core identity.
- [ ] Vocabulary, syntax, inclusive-language and prohibited-pattern guidance is actionable.
- [ ] Sample copy covers representative touchpoints.
- [ ] Rewrites preserve factual meaning and disclaimers.
- [ ] Unsupported claims, guarantees or testimonials are not introduced.

## Try it
> Define a verbal identity for a fictional climate-tech company and demonstrate it in a homepage hero, an error message and a customer-support reply.
