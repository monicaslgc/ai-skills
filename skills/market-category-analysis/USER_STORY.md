# User story: market-category-analysis

## Story
As a strategy lead, I want a category map so that the business understands conventions, substitutes and opportunity spaces.

## Scenario
The business is entering a market with unclear category boundaries.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Category and geography defined
- [ ] substitutes included
- [ ] dated evidence cited
- [ ] market size methodology disclosed
- [ ] conventions separated from sample-specific observations
- [ ] implications stated.

## Try it
> Map a fictional digital wellbeing category. Then ask for market size without credible data.
