# User story: bottleneck-and-flow-analyzer

## Audience
An operations lead or practitioner who needs to diagnose where work accumulates or loses flow, without assuming that the busiest step is automatically the root cause.

## Scenario
The user provides incomplete operational context and needs a repeatable, evidence-led artefact that can be reviewed before use.

## Acceptance criteria
- Workflow boundaries and unit of work are clear.
- Observed facts are separated from hypotheses.
- Proposed experiment has a baseline and guardrail.
- Recommendations consider downstream effects.
- Consequential changes remain subject to human approval.

## Try it
> Diagnose where work accumulates or loses flow, without assuming that the busiest step is automatically the root cause. Use fictional or approved data, include an edge case, and explain how to validate the result.
