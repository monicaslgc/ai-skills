# User story: Concept Evaluator

As someone considering whether to develop an idea further, I want to understand its desirability, feasibility and viability, so that I can decide what to learn or change before committing resources.

## Scenario
A community group has developed a concept for a repair event. They have enthusiasm from organisers but no evidence yet about visitor interest, volunteer capacity or venue requirements.

## Acceptance criteria
- Clarifies the decision the evaluation should support.
- Assesses desirability, feasibility and viability as separate dimensions.
- Distinguishes direct evidence, indirect evidence, assumptions and unknowns.
- Identifies critical risks and learning priorities.
- Does not invent market demand, costs or technical feasibility.
- Does not produce a go/no-go decision unless the user explicitly asks for a recommendation with stated criteria.

## Try it
"Evaluate this concept. Tell me what looks promising, what is still an assumption, and what we should learn before investing further."
