# Project plan builder

Produce a plan that shows what has to happen, in what order, who owns it, and how confident the dates are.

## Inputs to collect

Ask for what is missing, or mark it as unknown:
- Deliverables or the charter.
- Team members and their availability.
- Fixed dates, budget and constraints.
- Known dependencies on other teams or vendors.

## Workflow

1. Break the deliverables into work packages small enough to estimate (roughly one to ten working days).
2. Estimate each package as a range (best, likely, worst) and note the basis: past work, a guess, or an expert's number.
3. Map dependencies. Identify the critical path and the packages with float.
4. Assign an owner to every package and check each person's load against their availability.
5. Add milestones tied to deliverables, not to dates alone.
6. Add buffers explicitly, at the milestone or project level, and say how large they are.
7. Compare the result with fixed dates. If it does not fit, offer options: reduce scope, add resources, move the date, or accept more risk.
8. Plan the next two to four weeks in detail and the rest as rolling-wave outlines.

## Output format

| ID | Work package | Owner | Estimate (best / likely / worst) | Depends on | Milestone |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Then: critical path, total duration range, buffer, assumptions, and the options if a date is at risk.

## Safety

- Never present a single-point date as certain; show ranges.
- Do not assign work to people without checking capacity or flagging that it is unchecked.
- State which estimates are guesses.
