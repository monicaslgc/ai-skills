# User story: Constraint Remixer

As a person exploring a difficult challenge, I want to deliberately change one constraint at a time so that I can discover options hidden by the current framing without losing sight of what must remain true.

## Scenario
The user is planning a small exhibition with limited space, time and budget. They want unexpected formats but cannot change the venue's safety requirements.

## Acceptance criteria
- Separates fixed, negotiable, assumed and chosen constraints.
- Changes one or two variables deliberately.
- Protects non-negotiable requirements.
- Explains how each remix creates a new direction.
- Rechecks ideas against fixed constraints.

## Try it
"Remix the constraints of a small exhibition. Keep safety and venue requirements fixed; challenge the assumptions about space and time."
