# User story: ha-vacuum-routines

## Story

**As** a robot vacuum owner,
**I want** control, a routine and error handling for my vacuum,
**so that** the house stays clean without disturbing anyone.

## Scenario

My vacuum sometimes stops mid-clean and I do not find out until later.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Status, battery and errors are reported.
- [ ] Supported services are checked first.
- [ ] Commands are confirmed before running.
- [ ] A routine avoids quiet hours and occupied times.
- [ ] A stuck notification is proposed.
