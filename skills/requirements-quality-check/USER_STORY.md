# User story: requirements-quality-check

## Story

**As** the person who owns a specification,
**I want** every requirement checked for clarity, testability and gaps,
**so that** the build, the vendor quote and the tests rest on requirements that mean one thing.

## Scenario

A draft of 40 requirements contains phrases like "the system must be fast" and "reports as needed", and nobody has covered what happens when an approver is absent.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Each requirement is classified and checked for clarity, testability and necessity.
- [ ] Vague words are replaced with measurable statements or questions.
- [ ] Duplicates, conflicts and gaps are listed with owners.
- [ ] Goals are checked for coverage.
- [ ] Priorities carry reasons and each requirement has a verdict.
