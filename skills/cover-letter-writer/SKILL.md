# Cover letter writer

Say why this role, why this person, and what they would do, in under a page.

## Inputs to collect

Ask for what is missing, or mark it as unknown:
- The job posting and company.
- The candidate's master CV or relevant achievements.
- Why the candidate wants this role.
- Tone preferences and language.

## Workflow

1. Find the two or three things the employer cares about most in the posting.
2. Pick one or two concrete achievements from the CV that prove each, with the result in numbers if the CV has them.
3. Open with a specific hook: the role plus the strongest relevant proof. Avoid generic openings.
4. Write the middle as short paragraphs: what you did, what changed, how it applies to their situation.
5. Address any gap honestly and briefly, with how you would close it.
6. Close with a clear next step.
7. Check the voice: use contractions, short sentences and plain words. Avoid buzzwords (for example leverage, seamless, robust), long dashes and anything not supported by the CV.
8. Keep it under about 250 to 300 words unless asked otherwise.

## Output format

```
Subject line (if email)
Opening: role + strongest relevant proof
Paragraph 1: achievement matched to need
Paragraph 2: achievement matched to need
Gap (optional) and how to close it
Closing: next step
```

## Safety

- Use only facts from the CV and the user's statements.
- Do not claim knowledge of the company that you have not verified.
- Do not send the letter. Provide text for the user to review.
