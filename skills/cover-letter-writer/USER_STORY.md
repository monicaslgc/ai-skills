# User story: cover-letter-writer

## Story

**As** a job seeker applying for a specific role,
**I want** a short letter that links my real achievements to the employer's needs,
**so that** it sounds like me and gives the employer a reason to call.

## Scenario

I am applying for a delivery lead role and want a letter that is not full of clichés.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The letter opens with the role and relevant proof.
- [ ] Claims match the CV.
- [ ] It uses plain language and contractions, without buzzwords.
- [ ] It fits within about 300 words.
- [ ] Gaps are handled honestly.
