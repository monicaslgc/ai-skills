# Brand Consistency Auditor

## Purpose
Audit touchpoints against approved brand rules.

## Required inputs
- The user's objective and the decision or deliverable required.
- Relevant organisation, product, audience, market and language context.
- Existing source material, constraints and approved decisions.
- Unknowns; ask only for information that materially changes the work.

## Method
Gather guidelines and samples; score each criterion with evidence; separate critical violations from subjective opportunities; recommend prioritised fixes.

## Output
1. **Outcome:** the requested artefact or recommendation.
2. **Evidence:** source, date and confidence for material factual claims.
3. **Assumptions and unknowns:** separate from verified facts.
4. **Decisions needed:** owner and approval required, if any.
5. **Next step:** the smallest useful action.

## Quality and safety
- Preserve the user's terminology and supplied source facts; do not silently fill gaps.
- Separate evidence, interpretation, hypothesis and recommendation.
- Prefer primary sources and record URLs and access dates for research.
- Protect confidential, personal and commercially sensitive information; use synthetic examples in public artefacts.
- Do not penalise a touchpoint for rules that were never documented or approved.
- Follow the shared contracts in ../../kits/brand-strategy-identity/README.md when tools are available. If a tool is unavailable, state the limitation and provide a manual alternative.

## Consistency-specific decision rules

1. Obtain the current approved guideline version and identify the exact rules in scope before reviewing assets.
2. Create a touchpoint inventory. For each item record channel, audience, market, asset version and review date.
3. Classify each observation as: compliant, non-compliant, not assessable, or opportunity. Cite the exact rule and visible evidence.
4. Distinguish objective violations (for example, incorrect logo lock-up or prohibited claim) from subjective creative opportunities (for example, a tone that could be warmer).
5. Do not mark a touchpoint non-compliant when the guideline is silent, ambiguous or superseded. Flag the missing rule for governance.
6. Report severity, frequency and reach. Provide a concrete correction and identify whether the fix is local or requires a guideline update.
7. Never change approved brand rules while auditing. Route proposed rule changes to the brand owner as a separate decision.

## Boundary with adjacent skills

This skill checks execution against existing approved standards. Brand audit diagnoses the wider brand system, including whether the standards themselves are fit for purpose. When there is no approved baseline, recommend a brand audit or guideline-definition task rather than inventing one.
