# User story: create-cozy-gameplay-overlays

## Story

**As** a creator of cozy or ASMR gameplay videos,
**I want** vertical backgrounds, clean plates and title cards built from my own logo and style,
**so that** my gameplay looks polished and consistent without hand-editing every frame.

## Scenario

I have a landscape gameplay recording and need a 1080x1920 layout with space for my logo and a series label.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The background, clean plate, title card and gameplay layout are delivered as separate files.
- [ ] Only approved logo and background style are used, and missing source files are reported.
- [ ] Nothing critical sits in the platform interface areas.
- [ ] The exported files are checked at phone size before the task is called done.
