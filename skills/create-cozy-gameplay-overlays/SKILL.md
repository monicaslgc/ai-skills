# Create cozy gameplay overlays

## Source language

Use the real supplied gameplay, the creator's own logo and the background style they approved (for example a warm paper texture with subtle grain). Match a selected clean plate if one exists. Do not add a generic cozy room, extra props or a different pattern than the approved one.

Ask where the approved source files are before reusing them. If they are missing, work from newly supplied references and say so. Never claim to reuse a file you have not opened.

## Four separate deliverables

Do not collapse them into one flattened image when reusable assets were requested:
- **Background extension:** extend the exact screenshot to the target ratio.
- **Clean plate:** remove objects, text, UI and shadows, leaving only the background.
- **Title card:** the authentic logo plus a short series label on a clean plate.
- **Gameplay layout:** position the gameplay window, title and quiet UI areas.

## Starting layout at 1080x1920

Provisional measurements, not universal platform-safe zones. A platform screenshot and the real gameplay placement supplied by the creator override them.

| Area | Placement |
|---|---|
| Top interface | Nothing critical in the first 8 to 10% |
| 16:9 gameplay | about x=40 to 1040, y=270 to 835 |
| Logo + series label | about y=1120 to 1430, centred |
| Lower description/nav | keep about y=1500 to 1920 clear |
| Right control column | nothing critical in the rightmost ~12% |

The title goes below the gameplay and above the description; "at the bottom" does not mean under the platform caption. Never put labels over small gameplay details.

## Workflow

1. Inspect the source and choose the exact target size.
2. Outpaint or remove the requested objects.
3. Compare paper (or other background) texture and colour with the approved style.
4. Keep the gameplay as a separate original layer if generation touches the surrounding area.
5. Typeset the series label cleanly, using the label the creator selected.
6. Composite a phone preview.
7. Export the clean background and the title version separately.

Do not promise ASMR in an export that has loud music or narration.

Inspect the exported files at phone size before reporting done.

## Safety

- Use only artwork, logos and footage the creator has the right to use.
- Keep originals unchanged; work on copies with versioned filenames.
- Do not publish or send anything.
