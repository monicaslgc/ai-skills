# User story: media-quality-analyzer

## Story
**As** a creator with photos and video clips, **I want** a structured diagnosis of light and colour issues, **so that** I can choose a safe correction before batch editing.

## Synthetic scenario
A fictional clip contains a window-lit room, a warm lamp and a short exterior shot. One frame is bright, but the clip changes lighting halfway through.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] The analysis distinguishes known metadata from assumptions.
- [ ] Video diagnosis samples multiple frames and scenes.
- [ ] Exposure, white balance, clipping and saturation findings include evidence and confidence.
- [ ] Mixed lighting and HDR uncertainty are flagged.
- [ ] Recommendations are bounded and include validation.

## Try it
“Analyse these fictional sample media files for exposure, temperature, tint, contrast and clipping. Propose corrections but do not edit anything yet.”
