# User story: project-retrospective

## Story

**As** a team lead closing a project,
**I want** a blameless retrospective that ends in a few concrete actions,
**so that** the next project avoids this one's mistakes and repeats its strengths.

## Scenario

We shipped three weeks late and the team is tired. I want a useful retro, not a blame session.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The timeline compares planned and actual.
- [ ] Root causes are systems or decisions, not people.
- [ ] No more than three actions, each with an owner, date and success check.
- [ ] What went well is captured with a reason.
- [ ] A follow-up is scheduled.
