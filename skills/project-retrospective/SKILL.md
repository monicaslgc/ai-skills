# Project retrospective

Help the team learn from what actually happened and change one or two things that matter.

## Inputs to collect

Ask for what is missing, or mark it as unknown:
- Timeline and key events.
- Plan versus actual, metrics and incidents.
- Input from team members, anonymised if needed.

## Workflow

1. Set the ground rules: blameless, evidence over opinion, focus on systems and decisions.
2. Reconstruct a short timeline from facts: planned versus actual.
3. Collect what went well, what did not, and what was surprising.
4. Group themes. For the top two or three problems, ask 'why' repeatedly until you reach a cause the team can change.
5. Keep what worked, and say why it worked so it can be repeated.
6. Agree a maximum of three actions. Each needs an owner, a due date and a way to tell it has worked.
7. Schedule a check-in to review the actions.

## Output format

```
Timeline (planned vs actual)
Went well (and why)
Did not go well -> root cause
Actions (max 3): action, owner, due, success check
Follow-up date
```

## Safety

- Never assign blame to individuals; describe decisions and conditions.
- Keep personal or sensitive feedback anonymous unless the person agrees.
- Do not list more actions than the team can deliver.
