# User story: brand-workshop-facilitator

## Story
**As** a brand project owner, **I want** a facilitated workshop that helps stakeholders explore options and make explicit decisions, **so that** the team leaves with shared understanding, clear ownership and documented open questions.

## Scenario
A leadership and marketing team needs to agree on the audience and brand direction but has limited time and competing opinions.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] The workshop brief states the decision, participants, decision owner, duration and pre-work.
- [ ] The agenda is timeboxed and designed around the decision required.
- [ ] Questions are neutral and activities distinguish divergence from convergence.
- [ ] Evidence, opinions, assumptions and proposals are labelled separately.
- [ ] Dissent and unresolved topics are preserved rather than hidden behind a vote.
- [ ] Decisions are read back and assigned to a named approver.
- [ ] The output includes minutes, owners, due dates and approval status; no decision is treated as approved by silence.

## Try it
> Design a 90-minute positioning workshop for a fictional B2B consultancy with three founders who disagree on its target audience.
