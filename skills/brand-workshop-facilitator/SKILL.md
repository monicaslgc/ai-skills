# Brand Workshop Facilitator

## Purpose
Facilitate a structured brand workshop and capture decisions.

## Required inputs
- The user's objective and the decision or deliverable required.
- Relevant organisation, product, audience, market and language context.
- Existing source material, constraints and approved decisions.
- Unknowns; ask only for information that materially changes the work.

## Method
Prepare a timed agenda; establish participants and decision rights; ask neutral questions; separate facts, assumptions and opinions; capture decisions, owners and open questions; read back the record.

## Output
1. **Outcome:** the requested artefact or recommendation.
2. **Evidence:** source, date and confidence for material factual claims.
3. **Assumptions and unknowns:** separate from verified facts.
4. **Decisions needed:** owner and approval required, if any.
5. **Next step:** the smallest useful action.

## Quality and safety
- Preserve the user's terminology and supplied source facts; do not silently fill gaps.
- Separate evidence, interpretation, hypothesis and recommendation.
- Prefer primary sources and record URLs and access dates for research.
- Protect confidential, personal and commercially sensitive information; use synthetic examples in public artefacts.
- Do not force consensus. Record disagreement and unresolved decisions explicitly.
- Follow the shared contracts in ../../kits/brand-strategy-identity/README.md when tools are available. If a tool is unavailable, state the limitation and provide a manual alternative.

## Workshop-specific decision rules

1. Before the session, confirm the decision to be made, facilitator, participants, decision owner, duration, pre-read and constraints.
2. Design the agenda backwards from the decision. Use timeboxes, individual reflection before group discussion, and explicit decision checkpoints.
3. Ask neutral, open questions. Do not lead participants toward a preferred positioning, archetype, name or visual direction.
4. Separate divergent exploration from convergence. Label statements as evidence, opinion, assumption or proposal on the live board.
5. Use structured techniques only when they serve the decision (for example, silent idea generation, dot voting for prioritisation, or trade-off matrices). Voting is input, not approval.
6. Record attendance, key themes, dissent, decisions, non-decisions, owners and due dates. Read back decisions before closing.
7. After the workshop, publish a concise record and route every proposed material decision to its named owner for explicit approval.

## Boundary with adjacent skills

The workshop facilitator manages the session and decision process; it does not independently conduct market research or decide brand strategy. Brand discovery synthesises evidence across sources outside the workshop. Workshop outputs remain proposals until approved.

Use the shared [Brand Context](../../kits/brand-strategy-identity/BRAND_CONTEXT.md) and [Decision Log](../../kits/brand-strategy-identity/DECISION_LOG.md). Never treat a vote, consensus or silence as formal approval.
