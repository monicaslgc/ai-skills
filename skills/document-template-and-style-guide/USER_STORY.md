# User story: document-template-and-style-guide

## Story

**As** a team lead whose people write client documents in different ways,
**I want** templates and a short style guide,
**so that** our letters, reports and guides look and read consistently and are quicker to produce and review.

## Scenario

A small customer operations team sends monthly reports, handover packs and formal notices, each written from scratch, with different terms and date formats.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Templates exist for the few document types that matter, with a one-line instruction per section.
- [ ] Terms, dates and numbers follow one convention.
- [ ] A document control block and a review checklist are included.
- [ ] Examples use invented data.
- [ ] The guide has an owner and a review date.
