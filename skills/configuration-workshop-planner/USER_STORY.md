# User story: configuration-workshop-planner

## Story

**As** an implementation or customer operations manager,
**I want** to run configuration workshops that end with decisions and a working setup,
**so that** the customer is not waiting weeks for answers and nobody has to guess later why something is set that way.

## Scenario

A customer needs three roles, two approval rules and a catalogue set up. Their business lead and their IT contact are both available for half a day.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The purpose states what will be decided and what will exist at the end.
- [ ] A pre-read, questions and a draft configuration go out beforehand.
- [ ] The agenda has times, leads and outcomes.
- [ ] Decisions are logged with who decided and why.
- [ ] A configuration sheet and an action list with owners and dates exist.
- [ ] A follow-up message goes out within a working day.
