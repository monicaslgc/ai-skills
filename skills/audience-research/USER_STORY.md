# User story: audience-research

## Story
As a product or brand lead, I want evidence-based audience segments so that research and messaging focus on meaningful needs rather than stereotypes.

## Scenario
The team has analytics and a few interviews but no validated segmentation.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Segments are behaviour/need-led
- [ ] evidence and sample limits are recorded
- [ ] assumptions are labelled
- [ ] validation methods match uncertainties
- [ ] no unsupported demographic claims.

## Try it
> Segment a fictional meal-planning app from mixed interview and usage notes. Then repeat with no customer evidence and state what cannot be concluded.
