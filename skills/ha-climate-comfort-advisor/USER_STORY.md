# User story: ha-climate-comfort-advisor

## Story

**As** a homeowner with uneven comfort or high bills,
**I want** an analysis of how my heating or cooling behaves,
**so that** rooms are comfortable and the system does not waste energy.

## Scenario

The bedroom is cold in the morning while the living room is too warm.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Set points and actual temperatures are compared over time.
- [ ] Cycling and unnecessary running are identified.
- [ ] Conflicts between schedules and manual changes are checked.
- [ ] One change is tested at a time with confirmation.
- [ ] Safe temperature limits are respected.

## Try it

> Review my heating for the last three days and suggest how to make the bedroom warmer in the morning.
