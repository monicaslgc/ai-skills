# User story: workload-and-capacity-balancer

## Audience
An operations lead or practitioner who needs to help an operations lead balance incoming work, available capacity, priority and service commitments.

## Scenario
The user provides incomplete operational context and needs a repeatable, evidence-led artefact that can be reviewed before use.

## Acceptance criteria
- Demand and capacity use compatible units and periods.
- Assumptions and exclusions are visible.
- Skills and non-queue work are considered.
- No staffing or schedule change is treated as approved.
- Consequential changes remain subject to human approval.

## Try it
> Help an operations lead balance incoming work, available capacity, priority and service commitments. Use fictional or approved data, include an edge case, and explain how to validate the result.
