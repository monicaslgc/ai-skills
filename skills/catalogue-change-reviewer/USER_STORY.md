# User story: catalogue-change-reviewer

## Audience
A practitioner who needs to review catalogue imports and bulk edits for impact, reversibility, sku uniqueness and deletion risk.

## Acceptance criteria
- Source facts and assumptions are separated.
- Ambiguous or risky records are flagged rather than guessed.
- Validation and reviewer actions are explicit.
- Destructive or customer-visible changes require approval.

## Try it
> Review catalogue imports and bulk edits for impact, reversibility, SKU uniqueness and deletion risk. Use fictional data, flag missing facts, and include a review checklist.
