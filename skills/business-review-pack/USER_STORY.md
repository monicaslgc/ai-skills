# User story: business-review-pack

## Story

**As** the person who owns a customer relationship,
**I want** a short, honest review pack built around the customer's goals,
**so that** they see the value, trust us on the problems and agree the next steps, including at renewal.

## Scenario

A customer's quarterly review is next week. Usage grew in one team and stalled in another, and a sync incident last month was fixed after two days.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Starts with the customer's goals and shows evidence with baselines and trends.
- [ ] Adoption, support and incidents are summarised with causes and fixes.
- [ ] Risks and open items have owners and plans.
- [ ] Only shareable roadmap items are included.
- [ ] Next steps have owners and dates; minutes go out within a day.

## Try it

> Prepare the quarterly business review for this customer from the usage export, the incident list and the contract summary. Keep it to five slides and be honest about the sync incident.
