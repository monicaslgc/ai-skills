# Business review pack

Show the customer the value they get, the problems that were handled and the plan ahead, in one conversation.

## Inputs to collect

Ask for what is missing, or mark it as unknown:
- The customer's goals from onboarding or the last review, and any success metrics agreed.
- Usage and adoption data, support and incident history, delivery milestones and changes since the last review.
- Contract facts: scope, renewal date, open commercial topics, expansion interest.
- Who will attend on both sides, their roles and what each cares about; the time available.
- Roadmap items that matter to this customer and what can be shared.

## Workflow

1. Start from the customer's goals, not from your product. State each goal and the evidence of progress.
2. Show results in a few clear measures with a baseline and a trend. Explain how each was calculated. Say plainly when a measure is flat or worse.
3. Summarise adoption: who uses what, where usage is strong or weak and what you suggest (see [`adoption-review`](../adoption-review)).
4. Summarise support and incidents: volume, themes, notable incidents with cause and fix, response and resolution against agreed targets.
5. List what was delivered since last time and what is still open on both sides.
6. State risks and issues honestly with owners and plans, including anything the customer must do.
7. Share only the roadmap items that are committed or that you are allowed to discuss, and tie each to the customer's goals.
8. Propose next steps: actions, owners, dates, and any asks (training, access, a sponsor, a reference). Keep the commercial topic factual and brief.
9. Keep the pack to a few pages or slides, send it a few days before with the questions you want answered, and send minutes with actions within a day after.

## Rules

- Be honest about shortfalls; a review that only celebrates is not believed.
- Do not promise roadmap dates that are not committed.
- Do not show one customer's data to another; protect personal data.
- Every action leaves the meeting with an owner and a date.

## Output format

```
Customer / period / attendees
Goals and progress: goal | measure | baseline | now | trend | comment
Adoption and usage
Support and incidents
Delivered / open
Risks and issues: item | owner | plan
Roadmap relevant to the customer (shareable only)
Next steps: action | owner | date | asks
Questions to discuss
```

## Quality checks

- Goals come first and each has evidence.
- Measures have baselines and definitions.
- Shortfalls and risks are stated, with owners.
- Actions are agreed in the meeting and sent in writing.
