# User story: official-letter-and-notice-writer

## Story

**As** a customer operations or account manager,
**I want** a correctly structured official letter,
**so that** the recipient understands what changes and what to do, and the message stands up as a record.

## Scenario

A software provider must tell a customer, with the contractual notice period, that a feature will be retired and that the plan price will change at the next renewal.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The subject line names the matter and the reference.
- [ ] Facts, clauses, dates and amounts match the sources, and gaps are marked.
- [ ] The recipient knows what to do, by when, and whom to contact.
- [ ] Points needing legal or management approval are listed.
- [ ] A short cover message for the sending channel is included.
