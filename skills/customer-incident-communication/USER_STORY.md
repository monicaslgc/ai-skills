# User story: customer-incident-communication

## Story

**As** the person customers hear from when something breaks,
**I want** clear, honest messages during and after an incident,
**so that** customers keep their trust even when the service does not work as it should.

## Scenario

Booking confirmations are delayed for several customers since early morning. The cause is still unclear and the engineering team expects an update in two hours.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A first notice goes out early, before the cause is known, with the time of the next update.
- [ ] Every update has the same structure and plain wording.
- [ ] Uncertainty is stated; no fix time or cause is guessed.
- [ ] A resolution message and a post-incident report with owners and dates.
- [ ] Executive and technical versions where needed.
