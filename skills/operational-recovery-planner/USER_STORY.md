# User story: operational-recovery-planner

## Audience
An operations manager or business lead who needs to turn an agreed diagnosis into a realistic recovery plan with clear accountability and decision points.

## Scenario
The user supplies operational context that may be incomplete and needs a repeatable artefact that can be reviewed before action.

## Acceptance criteria
- Actions link to evidence or explicitly stated hypotheses.
- Each phase has a measurable verification point.
- Targets and dates are provided or clearly awaiting approval.
- No intervention is presented as guaranteed to work.
- Consequential decisions remain subject to accountable human approval.

## Try it
> Draft a phased operational recovery plan from this evidence. Separate stabilisation, diagnosis and corrective action; define review gates and metrics without inventing targets, owners or dates.
