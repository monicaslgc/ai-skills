# User story: capacity-planning-forecast

## Story

**As** a manager who must justify staffing,
**I want** a forecast of workload converted into staffing needs, with scenarios,
**so that** I can plan for busy periods and explain my request with numbers.

## Scenario

A seasonal peak is coming and I need to know whether the current team can cope.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Volume forecasts state their method and limits.
- [ ] Handling time and shrinkage are included.
- [ ] Required and available staff are compared per period.
- [ ] Expected, high and low scenarios are shown.
- [ ] Recommendations list the risk of each option.
