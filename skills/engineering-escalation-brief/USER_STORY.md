# User story: engineering-escalation-brief

## Story

**As** a customer operations or support specialist,
**I want** to escalate a customer problem to Engineering with everything they need,
**so that** it is investigated quickly and the customer gets honest updates.

## Scenario

A customer reports that confirmations stopped syncing at the start of the morning. Support has restarted the connection and checked their credentials, with no change.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A one-line summary naming the visible effect and the affected part.
- [ ] Impact, urgency and a severity proposal stated with facts.
- [ ] Steps to reproduce with expected and actual results.
- [ ] Evidence explained, free of secrets and unnecessary personal data.
- [ ] What was already tried and the scope are stated.
- [ ] A specific ask with a needed-by time and a customer update plan.
