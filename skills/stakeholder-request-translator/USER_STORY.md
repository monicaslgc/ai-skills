# User story: stakeholder-request-translator

## Story

**As** a product or operations person between the business and engineering,
**I want** to turn what a stakeholder asks for into a request engineers can build,
**so that** the result matches what the stakeholder needed and nothing is lost in translation.

## Scenario

A sales manager writes: "We need a faster way to approve discounts, it takes too long." Engineers cannot start from that sentence.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The original request is kept verbatim and traceable.
- [ ] The goal and a success measure are stated, separately from any proposed solution.
- [ ] Vague words became questions or concrete examples.
- [ ] Process, data, roles, integrations and edge cases are described.
- [ ] Acceptance criteria are checkable; assumptions are labelled.
- [ ] The stakeholder confirmed the plain-language read-back.
