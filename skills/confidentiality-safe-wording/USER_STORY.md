# User story: confidentiality-safe-wording

## Story

**As** a professional writing about work covered by confidentiality,
**I want** my achievements described without revealing confidential details,
**so that** my CV and portfolio are impressive and I stay inside my obligations.

## Scenario

I led an integration with a named partner organisation and a named supplier and want to describe it publicly.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Names, codenames, figures and people are removed or generalised.
- [ ] The verbs, scale and results remain.
- [ ] A change list is provided.
- [ ] Remaining risks are flagged.
- [ ] The rewrite cannot easily be traced to a specific matter.
