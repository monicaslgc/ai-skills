# User story: raid-log-manager

## Story

**As** a project manager running a weekly risk review,
**I want** a single log with scored, owned and dated items,
**so that** nothing slips through and the team talks about the right problems first.

## Scenario

A key supplier has been slow to confirm a delivery date and I need to decide whether it is a risk or an issue.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Items are classified as risk, issue, assumption or dependency.
- [ ] Risks are written as cause, event and effect.
- [ ] Each risk has a rating, response, owner and review date.
- [ ] The top five and overdue items are called out.
- [ ] No item is quietly downgraded.
