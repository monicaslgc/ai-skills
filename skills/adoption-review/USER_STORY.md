# User story: adoption-review

## Story

**As** a customer operations manager,
**I want** to understand why a customer's usage is lower than expected and what to do about it,
**so that** they reach the value they bought and are more likely to renew.

## Scenario

Three months after go-live, one customer team uses the product daily while another barely logs in. A business review is coming.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A value hypothesis based on the customer's goals.
- [ ] Usage compared by team, role, feature and period, with definitions.
- [ ] Causes of low engagement tied to evidence or to questions to ask.
- [ ] Interventions with owner, date and metric; a re-check date.
- [ ] A short, balanced summary for the customer.

## Try it

> Review this customer's adoption three months after go-live from the usage export and the ticket themes. Tell me why the second team is not using it and what to do.
