# Moodboard Art Direction

## Purpose
Create an art-direction brief and moodboard structure.

## Required inputs
- The user's objective and the decision or deliverable required.
- Relevant organisation, product, audience, market and language context.
- Existing source material, constraints and approved decisions.
- Unknowns; ask only for information that materially changes the work.

## Method
Translate strategy into visual territories; define imagery, composition, colour behaviour, typography mood, texture and exclusions; annotate references; compare territories against strategy.

## Output
1. **Outcome:** the requested artefact or recommendation.
2. **Evidence:** source, date and confidence for material factual claims.
3. **Assumptions and unknowns:** separate from verified facts.
4. **Decisions needed:** owner and approval required, if any.
5. **Next step:** the smallest useful action.

## Quality and safety
- Preserve the user's terminology and supplied source facts; do not silently fill gaps.
- Separate evidence, interpretation, hypothesis and recommendation.
- Prefer primary sources and record URLs and access dates for research.
- Protect confidential, personal and commercially sensitive information; use synthetic examples in public artefacts.
- Keep references traceable and distinguish existing work from proposed original design.
- Follow the shared contracts in ../../kits/brand-strategy-identity/README.md when tools are available. If a tool is unavailable, state the limitation and provide a manual alternative.

## Decision rules
- Translate approved strategy into 2–4 meaningfully different visual territories before selecting a direction.
- For each territory, define intended audience perception, visual principles, image subjects, composition, colour behaviour, typography character, texture and exclusions.
- Annotate every reference with source, creator where known, date and the specific attribute being referenced.
- Avoid collecting attractive images without a strategic rationale; explain why each reference belongs.
- Separate reference material, generated exploration and original proposed assets; do not imply ownership of third-party work.
- Check cultural representation, accessibility, image rights and feasibility for real production.
- Present trade-offs and open questions; a moodboard is exploratory until direction is explicitly approved.
