# User story: agent-charter-writer

## Story

**As** someone building or managing an AI assistant,
**I want** a charter that sets the agent's scope, access and approval rules,
**so that** everyone knows what it may do, who owns it and when it must stop and ask.

## Scenario

We want an agent to triage support emails and draft replies, but not send them.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Purpose, users, in-scope and out-of-scope tasks are stated.
- [ ] Tools and data are listed as read or change.
- [ ] The autonomy level is justified.
- [ ] Escalation rules and an owner are named.
- [ ] Limits and success measures are included.
