# Brand Stakeholder Interviews

## Purpose
Plan and synthesise stakeholder interviews for brand discovery.

## Required inputs
- The user's objective and the decision or deliverable required.
- Relevant organisation, product, audience, market and language context.
- Existing source material, constraints and approved decisions.
- Unknowns; ask only for information that materially changes the work.

## Method
Define objectives and sampling; write neutral prompts; capture evidence; code themes, disagreements and confidence; return implications and follow-up questions.

## Output
1. **Outcome:** the requested artefact or recommendation.
2. **Evidence:** source, date and confidence for material factual claims.
3. **Assumptions and unknowns:** separate from verified facts.
4. **Decisions needed:** owner and approval required, if any.
5. **Next step:** the smallest useful action.

## Quality and safety
- Preserve the user's terminology and supplied source facts; do not silently fill gaps.
- Separate evidence, interpretation, hypothesis and recommendation.
- Prefer primary sources and record URLs and access dates for research.
- Protect confidential, personal and commercially sensitive information; use synthetic examples in public artefacts.
- Do not treat a small internal sample as representative of customers.
- Follow the shared contracts in ../../kits/brand-strategy-identity/README.md when tools are available. If a tool is unavailable, state the limitation and provide a manual alternative.

## Decision rules
- Define learning objectives and decision relevance before recruiting; sample for role, influence and viewpoint diversity.
- Obtain informed consent, explain recording/notes use and retention, and minimise personal data.
- Use neutral open prompts and follow-up probes; avoid leading, compound or approval-seeking questions.
- Separate verbatim evidence, interviewer interpretation and participant recommendations.
- Code themes with counterexamples and dissent; note sample limitations and organisational power dynamics.
- Do not treat seniority, repetition or confidence as proof of customer truth.
- Deliver anonymised synthesis, evidence excerpts where authorised, disagreement map and questions requiring wider research.
