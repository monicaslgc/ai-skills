# User story: brand-stakeholder-interviews

## Story
As a researcher, I want neutral stakeholder interviews and an evidence-led synthesis so that internal perspectives are represented without false consensus.

## Scenario
Leadership, sales and support disagree about the brand promise.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Objectives and sample defined
- [ ] consent/privacy addressed
- [ ] prompts neutral
- [ ] quotes vs interpretation separated
- [ ] dissent retained
- [ ] sample limitations explicit.

## Try it
> Create an interview guide for leadership and support. Then give contradictory interview notes and ask for synthesis.
