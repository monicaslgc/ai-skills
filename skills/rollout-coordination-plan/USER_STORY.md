# User story: rollout-coordination-plan

## Story

**As** the person coordinating a launch across several organisations,
**I want** a clear plan of who does what, in which order and who decides,
**so that** blockers surface early and nobody waits for someone who did not know it was their turn.

## Scenario

A customer's rollout needs an engineering change, a partner connector and the customer's IT to open firewall rules, in three time zones, before a go-live in six weeks.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A shared definition of done.
- [ ] A responsibility table with one accountable person per task.
- [ ] A sequence with dependencies, dates and the critical path.
- [ ] Readiness gates with criteria and confirmers.
- [ ] A blocker log, a communication rhythm and an escalation path with names.

## Try it
