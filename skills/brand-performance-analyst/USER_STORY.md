# User story: brand-performance-analyst

## Story
As a marketing director, I want brand metrics interpreted against a baseline so that investment decisions are based on evidence.

## Scenario
Awareness and consideration surveys are available before and after a campaign.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Metric definitions, baseline, sample and period included
- [ ] outcomes separated from channel outputs
- [ ] attribution limitations explained
- [ ] no causation claimed without design
- [ ] missing data explicit.

## Try it
> Analyse before/after brand survey data. Then remove the baseline and ask for ROI and causal attribution.
