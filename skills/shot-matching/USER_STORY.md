# User story: shot-matching

## Story
**As** a creator editing a sequence, **I want** adjacent shots to feel coherent, **so that** changes in white balance and exposure do not distract from the story.

## Synthetic scenario
Two fictional shots in one kitchen have different camera white balance; a third shot at sunset is intentionally warmer.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Reference and target shots are identified.
- [ ] Each shot receives its own comparison.
- [ ] Intentional warm lighting is not automatically neutralised.
- [ ] The output contains a per-shot parameter table.
- [ ] Manual exceptions are listed.

## Try it
“Match these shots to the first reference while preserving the intentional sunset warmth. Give me settings and a review plan.”
