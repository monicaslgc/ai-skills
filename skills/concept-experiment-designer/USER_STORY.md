# User story: Concept Experiment Designer

As someone with an early concept and limited time or resources, I want to test one critical assumption with a small experiment, so that I can learn before committing to full development.

## Scenario
A team believes visitors would enjoy choosing between observing a repair, joining a short lesson or asking for help. They need to learn which parts are understandable and appealing before organising a public event.

## Acceptance criteria
- Defines a decision and one critical assumption.
- Poses a falsifiable learning question.
- Recommends a proportionate method and explains its limits.
- Includes a practical protocol and neutral prompts.
- Defines positive, negative and ambiguous signals.
- Addresses consent, privacy, accessibility, safety and approvals.
- Does not claim that a small concept test proves broad demand.

## Try it
"Design a small test for this concept. Help me learn whether the experience makes sense to the intended audience without leading participants."
