# Plan creator content

## Priorities

Fidelity to the current brief, then truthful claims, then the creator's likeness and authentic game art, then usable output, then reach experiments. Never trade accuracy or identity for hypothetical reach. Make a provisional plan with labelled assumptions rather than blocking on missing inputs or analytics.

## Research

Separate three jobs; a finding in one does not prove another:
- Game research: what is actually new, released, useful or changed.
- Format research: what creators publish and what viewers ask.
- Channel research: what works for this channel, from authorised real data only.

Evidence rules:
1. Use official game and developer material and official platform docs for facts. Use original creator uploads for format examples. Reposts are leads only.
2. Record access level per source: viewed, metadata only, official statement, community report, hypothesis. Never claim a whole video was watched from a snippet.
3. Record publication date and observation date separately. Record metrics only if visible, with the observation time; unknown stays unknown.
4. Describe timing and edit choices only after watching. Record an adaptation lesson, never a copied script or design.
5. Keep a source ledger: URL, title or owner, publication date, access date, claim supported, evidence level.
6. For a substantial audit aim for 15 to 25 unique examples across at least five formats, including smaller creators, without padding. Weak indexing on one platform is not evidence nobody posts there.
7. Never invent popularity, retention, comments, best posting times or "best-performing hashtags".

## Selecting ideas

Group by viewer promise (discovery, reveal, practical help, humour, relaxation, narrative project, comparison, challenge). Do not call an idea untapped unless the search could support it; call it a hypothesis to test. Rank by hook, channel fit, evidence of interest, footage readiness and effort (1 to 5 is judgement, not prediction). Decide: publish from existing material, film next, save, or skip.

Each selected idea needs: premise, viewer payoff, 3 to 5 beats, exact hook, required assets, audio role, approximate duration, platform treatment, effort and factual checks. Batch plans are grouped by recording session. Recheck trends close to use; no maintenance joke after maintenance ended.

## Long-form YouTube

A long video must earn its length (process, decisions, tests, comparison, coherent relaxation), not be Shorts glued together. Structures: pulling and testing; character build; outfit lookbook; tutorial; comparison; relaxation session. A starting range for builds is 8 to 16 minutes; otherwise choose from the useful footage, and never claim a length is proven for the channel.

The brief contains: viewer promise, target viewer, three genuinely different titles plus a recommendation, a thumbnail concept from available art, the opening 15 to 30 seconds, approximate chapters, must-capture shots, commentary prompts, sound plan, cuts, final payoff, dependencies, effort, and at least three distinct Short derivatives when repurposing. No invented chapters for an unfinished edit. Keep branded intros short; no loud meme transitions in relaxation content. Each derivative Short delivers a complete small payoff and never withholds the whole result to force a click. Preparing a linking suggestion is not changing a live upload.

## Calendars and campaigns

Statuses: proposed, ready to film, recorded, editing, ready, scheduled, published. Ready is not published. Group recording by assets and setup. Alternate outfit detail, reveal, joke and tutorial. A supplied challenge list is the authority: transcribe all prompts and dates and compare back; do not call it official without evidence.

## Reviewing performance

Use only supplied or authorised data. Record platform, metric, window, publish age, format and duration. Compare similar formats at similar ages, separate organic from paid, and account for production effort. Diagnose the failing stage:
- Low impressions: distribution or topic unknown; do not blame the thumbnail.
- Weak click-through: test the promise, title or thumbnail.
- Early drop: opening clarity or promise mismatch.
- Healthy watch time but small reach: uncertain; compare more.

These are hypotheses, not thresholds. Change one main thing per experiment, define success before publishing, and review at a consistent interval (for example 48 hours and 7 days). Claim no significance from uncontrolled comparisons.

## Safety

- Do not post, schedule, comment or message anyone.
- Do not log in to an account or share analytics outside the conversation.
