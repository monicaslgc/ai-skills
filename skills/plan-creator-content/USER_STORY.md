# User story: plan-creator-content

## Story

**As** a creator deciding what to film and publish next,
**I want** researched, honest plans, briefs and calendars,
**so that** I spend my time on content that fits my channel and avoid guesswork.

## Scenario

I have footage from one session and want an idea bank, a long-form brief and a performance review of last week's videos.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Claims are separated by evidence level, and unknown metrics stay unknown.
- [ ] Each selected idea has a premise, beats, hook, assets and effort.
- [ ] Long-form briefs are more than Shorts joined together.
- [ ] Performance diagnoses are labelled hypotheses, with one change per experiment.
- [ ] Nothing is posted, scheduled or changed on a live account.

## Try it

> Plan next week's content from these recordings and review why last week's video underperformed.
