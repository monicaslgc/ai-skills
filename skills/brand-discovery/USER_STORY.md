# User story: brand-discovery

## Story
**As** a brand or business lead, **I want** a structured discovery process that separates evidence from assumptions, **so that** strategic decisions are based on a reliable understanding of the organisation, audience and market.

## Scenario
A team is preparing a rebrand or new product launch but has fragmented internal knowledge, untested audience assumptions and incomplete market evidence.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] The decision to be informed and the discovery scope are explicit.
- [ ] Business, offer, audience, category, experience and existing-asset evidence are mapped.
- [ ] Each material insight has a source, date and confidence level.
- [ ] Internal beliefs, customer evidence and market evidence are not conflated.
- [ ] Unknowns and contradictions are prioritised with a proposed way to resolve them.
- [ ] The output is a discovery brief, not an unapproved positioning or creative concept.
- [ ] The handoff identifies the next strategic decision and its owner.

## Try it
> Prepare a discovery brief for a fictional SaaS company entering a second European market. Separate verified evidence, assumptions and research gaps.
