# User story: shift-schedule-planner

## Story

**As** a team lead who builds the weekly rota,
**I want** a schedule that covers demand, follows the rules and is fair,
**so that** the team trusts the rota and the service is covered.

## Scenario

Two people are on leave next week and a busy day needs extra cover.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Coverage is checked for every time block.
- [ ] Hard rules and soft preferences are separated.
- [ ] Each person is checked for hours and rest periods.
- [ ] Fairness of weekend and late shifts is shown.
- [ ] Conflicts are listed with options.
