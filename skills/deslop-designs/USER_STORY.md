# User story: deslop-designs

## Story

**As** a creator or designer who wants designs to feel hand-made, not generic,
**I want** a clear checklist that removes common AI-looking tells,
**so that** my designs are consistent and give specific, fixable feedback.

## Scenario

A redemption-code card looks cluttered with glow effects and default fonts, and I want it fixed.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The review names concrete problems (font, colour, icon, spacing), not just 'less AI'.
- [ ] No more than three decorations are left, and nothing covers the main content.
- [ ] Only brand fonts and one palette are used, and every glyph renders.
- [ ] The approved artwork is not altered.
- [ ] The result is checked for readability on a phone.
