# User story: brand-launch-planner

## Story
As a launch owner, I want an internal and external rollout plan so that teams, channels and assets are ready before launch.

## Scenario
A rebrand is approved but digital, legal and staff dependencies remain.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Internal/external readiness separated
- [ ] dependencies and owners mapped
- [ ] go/no-go and rollback defined
- [ ] approvals linked to versions
- [ ] dates remain tentative until authorised.

## Try it
> Plan a staged rebrand launch. Then ask to announce a date before the website and legal review are ready.
