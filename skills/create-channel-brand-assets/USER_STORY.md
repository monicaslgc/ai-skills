# User story: create-channel-brand-assets

## Story

**As** a gaming creator who needs consistent channel graphics,
**I want** banners, frames, cutouts, mascot art and component packs made from my approved artwork,
**so that** my channel looks recognisable and I can keep editing the assets later.

## Scenario

I want a new banner and a transparent cutout of a character from a screenshot, without losing the style I already use.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The exact channel name spelling and approved style are used.
- [ ] Source artwork and the creator's likeness are preserved, not redrawn.
- [ ] Transparent means real alpha, checked over light, dark and saturated backgrounds.
- [ ] Originals are untouched and outputs have versioned filenames.
- [ ] Editability is tested or clearly marked as unverified.
