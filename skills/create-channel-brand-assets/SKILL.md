# Create channel brand assets

## Identity and authenticity

- Use the exact spelling of the channel name supplied. Keep the style the creator approved (for example cute scrapbook with gaming elements, clean spacing, crisp cutouts, legible type, limited decoration). Keep a broader gaming identity; do not force every game logo into every asset.
- No universal palette or font: extract from the approved artwork. Game art uses the game's own colours.
- Treat references as a selection problem: which layout, palette, texture, lettering and mascot were actually approved. A previous assistant output in storage is not approval.
- Preserve source artwork as artwork. Keep the creator's likeness exactly as it appears in the supplied photo; never invent features or accessories.
- Keep originals unchanged; use versioned filenames; never call a flattened PNG "editable".

## Banners

Convert an existing graphic by preserving its artwork and layout language; extend and reposition, do not invent an unrelated banner. Verify the current YouTube banner size and safe areas at production time. Use a guide layer hidden on export. Preview mobile and other crops and make sure the channel name survives. Deliver the final banner plus an editable source if asked. Do not claim "same as source" without comparing.

## Intro, outro, frames, transitions

First identify the deliverable: full-screen intro, end card, intermission card, gameplay frame, lower third, stinger, decorative component. If an MP4 reference is supplied, inspect representative frames and the actual motion (entrances, easing, timing, exits, texture, type, sound); never infer from a filename or first frame. A transparent gameplay centre means real alpha, demonstrated over sample footage. For a mascot-free editable version, remove only the mascot and rebalance. Create only what was requested. A static card is never an "animated transition". Specify duration, entry, hold, exit, cut coverage, resolution, frame rate and alpha. H.264 MP4 does not carry alpha; if alpha is needed, pick a codec and verify editor support.

## Mascot and illustration

- **Mascot with accessories:** a remembered description is a checklist, not geometry. Inspect the real selected reference first; if the photo already shows the accessory, preserve it. Do not invent shapes.
- **Plush-style mascots:** round, soft, integrated features, simple face, consistent construction, readable expression; cuter and rounder when in doubt. Parts that look detached get rejected. Use real references for proportions, render something new, never copy an unrelated artist, never call it official merchandise. Keep the mascot on its own layer.
- **Creator illustrations and cards:** accepted references only; stylised art is a different mode from a photo edit. Card text and stats stay editable and aligned. Do not turn a past card detail (like a number) into a permanent claim.
- **Audio-only reaction doodles:** transcribe and timestamp the recording, build cards around real beats (hope, surprise, disbelief, failure, relief), recompose horizontal and vertical rather than stretching, keep text and counts editable, never present cartoon exaggeration as a real result, and do not repeat decorations the creator removed.

## Game background plates

Fidelity to the game. Study sky and ground gradients, light, texture, horizon and shapes, and whether patterns really exist; never invent grids, ornaments, sparkles or tiles. "Background only" removes characters, named text, UI, panels and foreground objects with their shadows, repairing the whole region with no halo or ghost rectangle. For time-of-day sets, use verified in-game names or the creator's own captures, match each independently, and never fake colour filters. Keep canvas size and alignment identical across sets meant as masks. Distinguish an opaque plate, an alpha mask and an overlay. Vertical extension keeps the real centre; blur only a deliberate backdrop, never the sharp gameplay.

## Cutouts, completion, composites

Inspect all appendages first. Produce a clean alpha preserving hair, fingers, edges, semi-transparent fabric and intentional particles. A white background or baked-in checkerboard is not transparency. Compare with the source (pose, face, hands, costume, extent) and check over white, black and a saturated colour for halos. Add small transparent padding. "Separate images" means one independent file per image; a contact sheet is only an extra preview. When completing a cropped character, rebuild only the missing part from official or supplied art, check silhouette, ornament symmetry and finger anatomy, and say it is a reconstruction. For 4:3, extend the canvas without stretching. For character-swap memes, replace the complete relevant anatomy, check leftover fins, duplicates and joints, and do not imply official art.

## Component and redemption-code packs

Cute reusable parts that reflect the channel, not a generic parchment kit. Consistent corners, outlines and shadows; clear negative space. Starting sizes at 1080 wide: background 1080x1920 (opaque), title ribbon 900x90, panel 960x640, bubble 700x130, code pill 860x110, mascot 150x150, plus divider and sparkle as needed. Use alpha PNGs for foreground parts with no white rectangles. Leave the title, greeting, game name and codes out of the art so they can be typeset. Test worst-case text length, row count, padding and bubble pointer. Show an assembly with clearly marked sample text, but deliver empty components separately. Real codes need verified characters, expiry, region and source; never use placeholders as rewards.

## Image brief and acceptance

Internally state: task, exact target and reference roles, protected content, canvas and alpha, palette from the selected source, layout hierarchy and empty areas, exact text or "no text", failure exclusions tied to the brief, acceptance test, outputs.

Gates: right source and version; right ratio with no stretching; identity and game art kept; removals complete; transparent means alpha and separate means separate files; text readable and spelled right; no invented patterns or objects; editability tested or marked unverified; approved elements survive; the exported file itself inspected.

## Safety

- Use only artwork, music and images the creator has the right to use.
- Do not present fan art or edits as official game or merchandise material.
- Do not publish, post or send anything without explicit approval.
