# User story: kpi-insight-writer

## Story

**As** an analyst or manager reporting results,
**I want** numbers turned into a concise, honest insight,
**so that** readers understand what happened and what to do.

## Scenario

Resolution time rose 20% last month and leadership wants an explanation.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The headline states the finding, size and so-what.
- [ ] Causes are separated from hypotheses.
- [ ] Caveats are stated.
- [ ] Actions have owners.
- [ ] The text is brief and fits the audience.
