# Kpi insight writer

Give readers the point first, with enough evidence to trust it and a clear next step.

## Inputs to collect

Ask for what is missing, or mark it as unknown:
- The data or report and the period.
- Targets and previous periods.
- The audience and what they decide.
- Known events that affect the numbers.

## Workflow

1. Find the story: the two or three changes that matter most, using comparisons with target, previous period and last year if seasonal.
2. Check the data: definitions, missing periods, outliers, and whether the change is bigger than normal variation.
3. Write a headline with the finding, the size of the change and the so-what.
4. Explain likely causes, separating what the data shows from hypotheses. Say how each hypothesis could be tested.
5. State caveats: small samples, definition changes, data quality.
6. Recommend actions with owners and the expected effect.
7. Choose the right chart or table, and keep text brief. Put detail in an appendix.

## Output format

```
Headline (finding, size, so-what)
Key numbers (vs target / previous)
Likely causes (data-backed vs hypotheses)
Caveats
Recommended actions (owner, expected effect)
```

## Safety

- Do not overstate certainty or imply causation from correlation.
- Do not include personal data about individuals in shared reports.
