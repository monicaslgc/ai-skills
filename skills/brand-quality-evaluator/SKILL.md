# Brand Quality Evaluator

## Purpose

Review a brand deliverable consistently without presenting subjective taste as objective fact or confusing a polished artefact with a strategically sound one.

## Required inputs

- Deliverable and version being evaluated.
- Approved brief, strategy, guidelines and intended audience/channel, where available.
- Relevant evidence and constraints.
- Evaluation purpose: formative feedback, approval support or regression testing.

If the approved brief or criteria are missing, state that the review is provisional and do not invent rules.

## Evaluation method

Score each applicable criterion from 0 to 4:

| Criterion | What to inspect |
|---|---|
| Strategic coherence | Does the work follow the approved strategy and positioning? |
| Audience relevance | Does it address evidenced audience needs and context? |
| Distinctiveness | Is it recognisable without unsupported uniqueness claims? |
| Clarity | Can the intended audience understand the message and action? |
| Evidence and claims | Are factual, comparative and performance claims substantiated? |
| Consistency | Does it follow the approved, versioned identity and voice rules? |
| Feasibility | Can the organisation produce, maintain and use it? |
| Accessibility | Are contrast, readability, language and inclusive use considered? |
| Risk and governance | Are rights, permissions, legal review and approval needs addressed? |

Use anchored scores: **0** critical failure; **1** major weakness; **2** partially meets; **3** meets; **4** strong and well evidenced. Mark criteria not applicable rather than forcing a score.

For every score, cite a concrete example from the deliverable or source rule. Separate objective violations from subjective recommendations. Identify critical failures that block release regardless of the average score.

## Output

1. Scope, artefact version and evidence reviewed.
2. Scorecard with rationale and examples.
3. Critical blockers and missing evidence.
4. Prioritised revisions with expected benefit and owner.
5. Decision: ready for review, revise, or blocked; never approve on behalf of the authorised owner.

## Safety

- Do not claim that a score predicts market performance.
- Do not infer audience preference from internal opinions.
- Do not penalise deviations from undocumented or unapproved rules.
- Record uncertainty and evaluator disagreement.
- Treat this rubric as a review aid, not a substitute for legal, accessibility or user testing.
