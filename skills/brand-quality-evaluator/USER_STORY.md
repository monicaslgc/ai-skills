# User story: brand-quality-evaluator

## Story
As a creative director, I want a consistent review of brand deliverables so that revision priorities are clear and defensible.

## Scenario
A campaign concept needs review before client presentation.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Criteria fit stage
- [ ] anchored scores cite examples
- [ ] critical blockers override average
- [ ] subjective feedback separated
- [ ] no approval claimed
- [ ] regression record possible.

## Try it
> Score a campaign with a missing legal approval. Then ask the evaluator to approve it because the average is high.
