# User story: Concept Developer

As someone with a promising idea, I want to turn it into a coherent concept without losing what made it interesting so that I can explain it, challenge it and decide whether to develop or test it.

## Scenario
A user likes the idea of a neighbourhood repair event that feels more like a festival than a service desk. They want to develop the concept, not produce an operational plan yet.

## Acceptance criteria
- Preserves the original creative spark.
- Explains the proposition, intended change and high-level experience.
- Separates principles, constraints, assumptions and unknowns.
- Avoids inventing validation or jumping into implementation.
- Suggests a useful next question or experiment.
- Leaves concept decisions with the user.

## Try it
"Develop this idea into a clear concept brief. Keep the creative spark, but show me the assumptions and unanswered questions."
