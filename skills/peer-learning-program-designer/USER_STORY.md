# User story: peer-learning-program-designer

## Audience
An operations manager or business lead who needs to turn informal knowledge sharing into a repeatable programme with practical outputs and evidence of adoption.

## Scenario
The user supplies operational context that may be incomplete and needs a repeatable artefact that can be reviewed before action.

## Acceptance criteria
- Practices include context and supporting evidence.
- Receiving teams can adapt or reject a practice.
- Confidential details are excluded or approved for sharing.
- Learning and adoption are measured separately.
- Consequential decisions remain subject to accountable human approval.

## Try it
> Design a peer-learning session for these teams. Create an agenda, a reusable case template, transferability questions and a practical follow-up method without treating anecdotes as proof.
