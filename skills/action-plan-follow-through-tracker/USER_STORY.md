# User story: action-plan-follow-through-tracker

## Audience
An operations manager or business lead who needs to turn a static action list into a reliable follow-through review without treating status labels as proof of impact.

## Scenario
The user supplies operational context that may be incomplete and needs a repeatable artefact that can be reviewed before action.

## Acceptance criteria
- Status rules and review date are explicit.
- Missing data is flagged, not guessed.
- Completion and outcome are distinguished.
- Escalations follow supplied policy or are marked provisional.
- Consequential decisions remain subject to accountable human approval.

## Try it
> Review this operational action log. Identify overdue, blocked and evidence-missing actions, distinguish task completion from outcome, and prepare a concise follow-up agenda without inventing owners or dates.
