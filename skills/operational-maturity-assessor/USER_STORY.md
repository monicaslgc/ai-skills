# User story: operational-maturity-assessor

## Audience
An operations manager or business lead who needs to help teams understand how consistently operational practices are defined, executed, measured and improved.

## Scenario
The user supplies operational context that may be incomplete and needs a repeatable artefact that can be reviewed before action.

## Acceptance criteria
- Criteria are defined and visible before scoring.
- Every assigned level cites observable evidence.
- Unknowns remain unknown rather than being guessed.
- No certification or universal benchmark is implied.
- Consequential decisions remain subject to accountable human approval.

## Try it
> Assess operational maturity from this evidence. Define or apply explicit criteria, cite observable practices, mark unknowns and produce a proportionate improvement roadmap without claiming certification.
