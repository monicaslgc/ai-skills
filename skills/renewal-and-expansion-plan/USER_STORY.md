# User story: renewal-and-expansion-plan

## Story

**As** the person who looks after a customer's success,
**I want** a renewal plan that starts at the notice deadline and rests on shown value,
**so that** renewals do not come as a surprise and growth conversations rest on real needs.

## Scenario

A customer's contract renews in four months with a two-month notice period. Usage grew in one team, a sponsor changed and there was an incident last quarter.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The renewal date and the earlier notice deadline are dated, with checkpoints.
- [ ] A risk assessment with reasons and unknowns.
- [ ] A value story using the customer's own goals and numbers.
- [ ] Decision-makers, objections and answers are prepared.
- [ ] An expansion hypothesis based on evidence, and a handover to the account owner.
