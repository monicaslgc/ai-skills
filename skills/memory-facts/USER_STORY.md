# User story: memory-facts

## Story

**As** someone who stores structured facts and preferences about my own setup,
**I want** to see what the assistant knows about me, my projects and my systems,
**so that** I can check it is accurate and personalise future answers.

## Scenario

Before asking for recommendations, I want to confirm the assistant has my preferences right.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Facts are grouped by subject and preferences by category.
- [ ] Empty categories are skipped.
- [ ] Outdated or wrong facts are pointed out so I can correct them.
- [ ] Sensitive details are shown only to me, in this conversation.
