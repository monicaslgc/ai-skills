# User story: divergent-ideation

## Audience
A person or team that wants to explore possible approaches before selecting one.

## Scenario
The user has a challenge and needs a broad landscape of possibilities, not a premature recommendation.

## Acceptance criteria
- The assistant identifies the intended axis of exploration.
- It chooses suitable ideation techniques rather than applying all techniques mechanically.
- Ideas differ in underlying mechanism or framing.
- Speculative ideas are allowed and labelled.
- The assistant does not rank or discard ideas unless asked.
- The output makes it easy to select ideas for another round.

## Try it
> Give me ten distinct approaches to make a public library feel more welcoming to teenagers. Avoid assuming the answer needs to be an app or a social media campaign.
