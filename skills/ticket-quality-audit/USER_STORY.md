# User story: ticket-quality-audit

## Story

**As** a support lead running quality reviews,
**I want** a fair scorecard with calibrated reviewers,
**so that** feedback is consistent and leads to real improvement.

## Scenario

Two reviewers score the same ticket very differently.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Criteria have clear score descriptions.
- [ ] Samples are fair and the size is stated.
- [ ] Scores cite evidence.
- [ ] Calibration is part of the process.
- [ ] Findings lead to coaching and process actions.
