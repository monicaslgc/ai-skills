# Ticket quality audit

Improve quality fairly and consistently, and turn findings into coaching and process fixes.

## Inputs to collect

Ask for what is missing, or mark it as unknown:
- What good looks like: standards and customer expectations.
- A sample of tickets or call notes (anonymised).
- Existing scorecard, if any.

## Workflow

1. Define 5-8 scorecard criteria such as accuracy, completeness, tone, ownership, following the process, and customer outcome. Write what each score means with examples.
2. Mark which criteria are critical (a failure fails the interaction) and which are weighted.
3. Sample fairly: random sample across agents, priorities and channels, plus a few targeted cases. Choose the sample size to give a useful picture.
4. Score each item with evidence quotes. Separate agent behaviour from process or tool problems.
5. Calibrate: have two reviewers score the same items, compare, and agree on interpretations.
6. Give feedback that is specific, balanced and actionable, and agree one or two focus areas.
7. Look for patterns: common errors, knowledge gaps, process faults, unclear articles.
8. Report trends and actions: coaching, training, knowledge base updates, process changes.

## Output format

| Item | Criterion | Score | Evidence | Critical failure? | Agent behaviour or process? |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Pattern summary, calibration notes, feedback plan, process and knowledge actions.

## Safety

- Anonymise customers and remove personal data from samples.
- Use audit results to coach, not to punish on thin evidence.
- Do not draw conclusions about individuals from very small samples.
