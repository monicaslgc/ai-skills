# Brand Identity Prism

## Purpose
Use a six-facet identity prism to test brand coherence.

## Required inputs
- The user's objective and the decision or deliverable required.
- Relevant organisation, product, audience, market and language context.
- Existing source material, constraints and approved decisions.
- Unknowns; ask only for information that materially changes the work.

## Method
Assess physique, personality, culture, relationship, reflection and self-image; populate each facet from evidence; identify contradictions and missing evidence; derive implications.

## Output
1. **Outcome:** the requested artefact or recommendation.
2. **Evidence:** source, date and confidence for material factual claims.
3. **Assumptions and unknowns:** separate from verified facts.
4. **Decisions needed:** owner and approval required, if any.
5. **Next step:** the smallest useful action.

## Quality and safety
- Preserve the user's terminology and supplied source facts; do not silently fill gaps.
- Separate evidence, interpretation, hypothesis and recommendation.
- Prefer primary sources and record URLs and access dates for research.
- Protect confidential, personal and commercially sensitive information; use synthetic examples in public artefacts.
- Do not fill facets with generic adjectives or present the model as validated research.
- Follow the shared contracts in ../../kits/brand-strategy-identity/README.md when tools are available. If a tool is unavailable, state the limitation and provide a manual alternative.

## Decision rules
- Use the six facets as a diagnostic lens: physique, personality, culture, relationship, reflection and self-image.
- For each facet, cite the evidence and distinguish intended identity from customer-perceived image.
- Do not infer reflection (stereotypical user) or self-image (how users feel) from internal stakeholder opinions alone.
- Identify tensions between facets and test whether they are meaningful strategic choices or simply inconsistent execution.
- Avoid filling every facet with generic adjectives; translate each into observable behaviours, experience cues or language.
- Present the prism as a synthesis framework, not a validated psychometric instrument or research substitute.
- Deliver a six-facet matrix, evidence gaps, contradictions and implications for strategy.
