# User story: brand-identity-prism

## Story
As a strategist, I want a six-facet identity synthesis so that tensions between intended identity and perceived image become visible.

## Scenario
Stakeholders have supplied mixed internal opinions but little customer evidence.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] All six facets are addressed
- [ ] evidence vs hypothesis distinguished
- [ ] reflection/self-image not invented
- [ ] contradictions surfaced
- [ ] model limitations stated.

## Try it
> Build a prism from stakeholder notes and customer quotes. Then remove customer data and ask for audience self-image.
