# User story: daily-operations-review

## Audience
An operations lead or practitioner who needs to prepare a concise daily review of operational health, exceptions, decisions and next actions.

## Scenario
The user provides incomplete operational context and needs a repeatable, evidence-led artefact that can be reviewed before use.

## Acceptance criteria
- Every material statement has a source or is labelled as an assumption.
- Targets are only used when provided.
- Actions are specific and ownership is not invented.
- High-impact exceptions are visible and not buried in routine updates.
- Consequential changes remain subject to human approval.

## Try it
> Prepare a concise daily review of operational health, exceptions, decisions and next actions. Use fictional or approved data, include an edge case, and explain how to validate the result.
