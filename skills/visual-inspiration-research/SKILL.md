# Visual Inspiration Research

## Purpose
Find visual references based on strategy and personality.

## Required inputs
- The user's objective and the decision or deliverable required.
- Relevant organisation, product, audience, market and language context.
- Existing source material, constraints and approved decisions.
- Unknowns; ask only for information that materially changes the work.

## Method
Convert approved traits into search terms; research direct, adjacent and unrelated categories; collect references with source and rationale; identify overused conventions.

## Output
1. **Outcome:** the requested artefact or recommendation.
2. **Evidence:** source, date and confidence for material factual claims.
3. **Assumptions and unknowns:** separate from verified facts.
4. **Decisions needed:** owner and approval required, if any.
5. **Next step:** the smallest useful action.

## Quality and safety
- Preserve the user's terminology and supplied source facts; do not silently fill gaps.
- Separate evidence, interpretation, hypothesis and recommendation.
- Prefer primary sources and record URLs and access dates for research.
- Protect confidential, personal and commercially sensitive information; use synthetic examples in public artefacts.
- Do not copy assets or present a reference board as a finished identity.
- Follow the shared contracts in ../../kits/brand-strategy-identity/README.md when tools are available. If a tool is unavailable, state the limitation and provide a manual alternative.

## Decision rules
- Start from approved brand traits and a precise research question; translate each trait into observable visual signals.
- Search direct competitors, adjacent categories and unrelated references separately, labelling each source type.
- Record source URL, creator, date, context and the exact visual element being referenced.
- Identify recurring conventions, visual clichés and underused codes; do not mistake novelty for strategic fit.
- Respect copyright and licensing: references are for analysis, not permission to reproduce.
- Avoid copying a single brand's distinctive trade dress, logo or campaign composition.
- Deliver a curated reference set with rationale, patterns, exclusions and questions for art direction.
