# User story: statement-of-work-drafter

## Story

**As** a project or account manager,
**I want** a clear scope document for a customer project,
**so that** both sides agree what is delivered, by whom and when before work starts.

## Scenario

A customer wants to connect their booking system to an invoicing tool and train two administrators; requirements were agreed in a workshop but nothing is written down yet.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Deliverables have measurable acceptance criteria.
- [ ] Exclusions, assumptions and customer responsibilities are explicit.
- [ ] Timeline, acceptance and change control are described.
- [ ] Price and legal clauses are placeholders marked for sales and legal.
- [ ] Open points are listed.
