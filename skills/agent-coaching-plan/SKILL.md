# Agent coaching plan

Help someone improve with respect and a clear plan, based on evidence rather than impressions.

## Inputs to collect

Ask for what is missing, or mark it as unknown:
- Relevant examples and data about the person's work.
- The role expectations.
- The person's goals and context, as shared by them.
- Previous feedback and actions.

## Workflow

1. Gather evidence: specific examples, quality results and outcomes. Separate facts from assumptions and check for process causes.
2. Identify strengths to build on and one or two focus areas with the biggest impact.
3. Plan the conversation: open with the purpose, ask for their view first, share observations as examples, listen, agree on focus.
4. Co-create the plan: a goal in observable terms, actions, practice opportunities, resources, support from the manager, and dates.
5. Define how progress will be seen and when you will review it.
6. Document the agreed points briefly and share them with the person.
7. Follow up: recognise progress, adjust the plan and escalate to HR processes only when the policy requires it.

## Output format

```
Evidence (facts and examples)
Strengths
Focus areas (max 2)
Conversation outline: opening, their view, examples, agreement
Plan: goal, actions, support, dates
Review date and signs of progress
```

## Safety

- Keep personal and performance information confidential.
- Do not make decisions on employment status; follow the organisation's formal process with HR.
- Use placeholders in shared examples.
