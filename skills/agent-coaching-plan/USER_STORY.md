# User story: agent-coaching-plan

## Story

**As** a team lead preparing a one-to-one,
**I want** a coaching conversation and plan based on evidence,
**so that** the person knows what to improve and feels supported.

## Scenario

A capable team member has had several quality dips this month.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Evidence is specific and fact-based.
- [ ] Strengths and a maximum of two focus areas are named.
- [ ] The person's view is part of the conversation.
- [ ] Goals are observable and dated.
- [ ] A follow-up is scheduled.
