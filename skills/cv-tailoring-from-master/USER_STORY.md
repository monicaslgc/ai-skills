# User story: cv-tailoring-from-master

## Story

**As** a job seeker with a long master CV,
**I want** each application tailored from the master without exaggeration,
**so that** my CV matches the role and every statement is true.

## Scenario

A posting asks for project management and requirements skills, and my master CV is four pages.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Must-haves are mapped to CV evidence or listed as gaps.
- [ ] Nothing is added that is not in the master CV.
- [ ] Length and format fit the target.
- [ ] A list of cuts and gaps is provided.
- [ ] Confidential names are generalised.
