# User story: customer-onboarding-project-plan

## Story

**As** the person responsible for a new customer,
**I want** an onboarding plan with owners, dates and dependencies on both sides,
**so that** the customer reaches go-live on time and nothing waits for someone who did not know it was theirs.

## Scenario

A customer signed on Monday with a target go-live in eight weeks. They need two integrations, data to be loaded and training for three teams in two time zones.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Goals become measurable success criteria with dates.
- [ ] Scope states what is in, out and optional.
- [ ] Every milestone has one owner, a date and its dependencies; the critical path is marked.
- [ ] The customer's tasks are in the plan.
- [ ] Risks carry a mitigation and an early-warning sign.
- [ ] Go-live criteria and the status rhythm are agreed.
