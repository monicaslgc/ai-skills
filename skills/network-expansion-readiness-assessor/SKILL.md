# Network Expansion Readiness Assessor

## Purpose
Identify what must be ready before a business expands its operational network.

## Procedure
1. Confirm the proposed expansion scope, operating model, timeframe and decision authority.
2. Assess supplied evidence across leadership bandwidth, role clarity, documented processes, training, systems, reporting, support capacity and controls.
3. Record evidence, gaps, dependencies and confidence for each dimension; mark absent evidence as unknown.
4. Separate hard prerequisites from desirable capabilities and assumptions that need validation.
5. Identify relevant risks such as support dilution, inconsistent service, reporting gaps and overloaded central teams.
6. Propose readiness gates, evidence needed to pass each gate and actions to close gaps.
7. Summarise options and decisions for leadership; do not make the expansion decision automatically.

## Guardrails
Do not claim expansion will succeed or invent financial forecasts, legal prerequisites or capacity thresholds. A readiness assessment is decision support, not a guarantee.

## Output contract
- Expansion scope and assumptions
- Readiness matrix by dimension
- Prerequisites, gaps and dependencies
- Proposed readiness gates
- Risks and leadership decisions required

## Acceptance checks
- Unknown evidence is not scored as ready.
- Prerequisites are separated from preferences.
- Thresholds are supplied or proposed for approval.
- The final go/no-go decision remains with authorised leadership.

## Example prompt
"Assess readiness for this proposed network expansion. Build an evidence-based readiness matrix, separate prerequisites from desirable capabilities, identify gaps and define decision gates for leadership approval."

## Evaluation
Pass when claims are evidence-based, uncertainty is visible and approval boundaries are respected. Fail when facts, targets or outcomes are invented.
