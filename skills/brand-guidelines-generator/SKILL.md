# Brand Guidelines Generator

## Purpose
Produce usable brand guidelines from approved decisions.

## Required inputs
- The user's objective and the decision or deliverable required.
- Relevant organisation, product, audience, market and language context.
- Existing source material, constraints and approved decisions.
- Unknowns; ask only for information that materially changes the work.

## Method
Compile strategy, logo usage, colour, typography, imagery, layout, voice, accessibility, examples, asset links and governance; mark pending decisions; include version and owner.

## Output
1. **Outcome:** the requested artefact or recommendation.
2. **Evidence:** source, date and confidence for material factual claims.
3. **Assumptions and unknowns:** separate from verified facts.
4. **Decisions needed:** owner and approval required, if any.
5. **Next step:** the smallest useful action.

## Quality and safety
- Preserve the user's terminology and supplied source facts; do not silently fill gaps.
- Separate evidence, interpretation, hypothesis and recommendation.
- Prefer primary sources and record URLs and access dates for research.
- Protect confidential, personal and commercially sensitive information; use synthetic examples in public artefacts.
- Never fabricate logo files, colour values, font licences or approved rules.
- Follow the shared contracts in ../../kits/brand-strategy-identity/README.md when tools are available. If a tool is unavailable, state the limitation and provide a manual alternative.

## Decision rules
- Treat the approved Brand Context and decision log as inputs; include only Approved or Locked decisions as normative rules.
- Mark unresolved content as open/pending and do not fill gaps with invented colour values, fonts, logo variants or tone rules.
- Organise guidelines by task: identify, create, apply, check and request help. Include examples and anti-examples.
- Specify exact values only when supported by approved source assets (colour codes, font names/weights, spacing, clear space, minimum size).
- Include accessibility, localisation, licensing, asset links, governance owner, version, effective date and change history.
- Validate internal consistency between visual rules, voice rules, examples and templates.
- Output a publication-ready structure plus a list of missing approvals; never label draft guidance as final.
