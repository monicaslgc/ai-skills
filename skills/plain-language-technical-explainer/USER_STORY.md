# User story: plain-language-technical-explainer

## Story

**As** the person who sits between engineers and the business,
**I want** to explain technical matters in plain language without losing accuracy,
**so that** stakeholders can decide quickly and trust what they are told.

## Scenario

A nightly import failed twice and a fix needs either a quick patch with a risk of recurrence or a two-week redesign. The department head needs to choose before Friday.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The message leads with what it means for the reader and what is needed from them.
- [ ] Options are compared on cost, time, risk and trade-offs, with a recommendation.
- [ ] Confirmed, likely and unknown facts are separated.
- [ ] Next steps and when the reader will hear again are stated.
- [ ] A short and a one-page version exist; an engineer confirms the accuracy.
