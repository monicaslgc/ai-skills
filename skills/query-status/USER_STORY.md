# User story: query-status

## Story

**As** a smart-home owner,
**I want** to ask about the state, history and health of my devices,
**so that** I can find out what happened and what is broken without digging through dashboards.

## Scenario

A room was cold overnight. I ask what the heating did between midnight and 6 am and which devices are unavailable.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The assistant returns current states and history in a short, readable summary.
- [ ] Unavailable or unknown devices are listed with the likeliest cause.
- [ ] It does not change any device while answering a question.
- [ ] It says clearly when data is missing instead of guessing.
