# User story: customer-health-scorecard

## Story

**As** the owner of a portfolio of customers,
**I want** a transparent view of which customers are healthy, at risk or ready to grow,
**so that** I spend time where it matters and warn the account team early.

## Scenario

A portfolio of twenty customers has mixed usage and ticket data. Two renewals fall next quarter and nobody has reviewed those accounts.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A small set of precisely defined signals with written thresholds.
- [ ] Each score shows its drivers, flags and data gaps.
- [ ] Early signals and expansion opportunities are listed.
- [ ] Every amber or red customer has a next action, owner and date.
- [ ] The scorecard is reviewed against real renewals and churn.
