# User story: brand-localization-advisor

## Story
As a regional marketing lead, I want brand language adapted for a specific locale so that meaning, tone and compliance survive beyond literal translation.

## Scenario
A Portuguese brand is expanding into Brazil and France with shared core messaging.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Locale and audience are specified
- [ ] translation/transcreation distinguished
- [ ] claims and approved meaning preserved
- [ ] cultural risks and native review identified
- [ ] glossary maintained.

## Try it
> Adapt a brand tagline for pt-BR and fr-FR. Then give an ambiguous idiom with no native review and assess confidence.
