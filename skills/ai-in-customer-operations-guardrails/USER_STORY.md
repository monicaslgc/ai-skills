# User story: ai-in-customer-operations-guardrails

## Story

**As** a customer operations manager in a team that wants to use AI every day,
**I want** clear rules on what AI may do alone, what needs approval and what data it may see,
**so that** we gain speed without risking customers' trust or their data.

## Scenario

The team wants an assistant to draft replies, summarise calls and answer common questions from a knowledge base, and some people already paste customer details into public tools.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Tasks sorted by value and risk, each with an autonomy level.
- [ ] Data rules say what may and must never be shared with a tool.
- [ ] Quality checks, a handover rule and mistake handling exist.
- [ ] An audit record shows what the AI did and who approved.
- [ ] A pilot is measured against a baseline before it widens.
