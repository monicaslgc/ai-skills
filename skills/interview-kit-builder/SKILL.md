# Interview kit builder

Make interviews comparable and fair by asking the same relevant questions and scoring against clear criteria.

## Inputs to collect

Ask for what is missing, or mark it as unknown:
- The role description and must-have competencies.
- Interview stages and who will interview.
- Seniority and context.
- Legal or company constraints on questions.

## Workflow

1. List 5-7 competencies that matter for the role and define what good looks like for each at this level.
2. Write for each competency one behavioural question (what did you do) and one situational question (what would you do), plus two probing follow-ups.
3. Add a short role-specific exercise or case if it reflects real work.
4. Create a scoring rubric: for each competency, descriptions for strong, acceptable and weak answers, scored independently of other competencies.
5. Plan the logistics: who asks what, time per section, and candidate questions at the end.
6. Define the decision process: each interviewer scores before discussing, then compare evidence, not impressions.
7. Remove or avoid questions about protected characteristics or irrelevant personal topics.

## Output format

| Competency | What good looks like | Behavioural question | Situational question | Probes | Strong / OK / Weak |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Exercise, interview plan, decision process.

## Safety

- Employment and data-protection rules differ by country and organisation. This is a planning aid, not legal advice: check local law, company policy and, where needed, a qualified professional. Treat people fairly and avoid discriminatory criteria.
- Keep candidate information confidential and use placeholders in shared examples.
- Never score candidates on protected characteristics.
