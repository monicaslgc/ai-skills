# User story: Creative Room Round Facilitator

As a participant in a creative session, I want each Creative Room round to have a clear purpose and meaningful interaction so that the process feels like facilitated thinking rather than a list of personas.

## Scenario
The user has six rough ideas and asks the room to challenge the framing, not to choose a winner.

## Acceptance criteria
- Defines a round objective.
- Selects a relevant subset of perspectives.
- Creates at least one useful interaction between contributions.
- Does not invent conflict, force consensus or rank ideas without request.
- Leaves the user with a clear opportunity to redirect.

## Try it
"Run a Creative Room reset round. We are repeating ourselves; change the question rather than generating more of the same."
