# User story: management-review-pack-builder

## Audience
An operations manager or business lead who needs to prepare a decision-oriented management pack that helps leaders act without obscuring uncertainty or data limitations.

## Scenario
The user supplies operational context that may be incomplete and needs a repeatable artefact that can be reviewed before action.

## Acceptance criteria
- The pack leads with decisions, not a raw status dump.
- Numbers reconcile to the supplied sources.
- Targets, RAG labels and owners are grounded.
- Risks and uncertainty are not buried.
- Consequential decisions remain subject to accountable human approval.

## Try it
> Build a concise management review pack from the supplied validated data. Highlight decisions required, material variances, risks and follow-up actions; flag missing or non-comparable evidence.
