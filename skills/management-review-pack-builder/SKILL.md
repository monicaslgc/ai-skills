# Management Review Pack Builder

## Purpose
Prepare a decision-oriented management pack that helps leaders act without obscuring uncertainty or data limitations.

## Procedure
1. Confirm audience, review period, operating objectives, decision rights and preferred length.
2. Inventory available data and verify KPI definitions, reporting dates, source and status of prior actions.
3. Summarise results against supplied targets or comparable baselines; flag non-comparable metrics and missing evidence.
4. Select only material trends, exceptions, risks and opportunities, explaining why each matters to the decision.
5. Distinguish decisions already made, decisions required, recommendations and information-only updates.
6. Create a concise action log using only supplied or explicitly confirmed owners and dates.
7. Add an appendix of definitions, assumptions and data caveats where needed to make the pack auditable.
8. Run a final check that every recommendation has evidence, a decision owner and a stated uncertainty where relevant.

## Guardrails
Do not make data look more certain or positive than it is. Never invent a target, RAG status, owner, commitment or executive decision. Label illustrative forecasts and assumptions; do not imply a checklist or pack proves compliance.

## Output contract
- Executive summary and decisions required
- Performance snapshot with definitions
- Material variances and evidence
- Risks, exceptions and opportunities
- Action log and overdue commitments
- Data caveats and appendix

## Acceptance checks
- The pack leads with decisions, not a raw status dump.
- Numbers reconcile to the supplied sources.
- Targets, RAG labels and owners are grounded.
- Risks and uncertainty are not buried.

## Example prompt
"Build a concise management review pack from the supplied validated data. Highlight decisions required, material variances, risks and follow-up actions; flag missing or non-comparable evidence."

## Evaluation
Pass if the output follows the contract, grounds material claims in supplied evidence, handles at least one edge case, labels uncertainty and proposes a concrete validation or follow-up step. Fail if it invents data or bypasses an approval boundary.
