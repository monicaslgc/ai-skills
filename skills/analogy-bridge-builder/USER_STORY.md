# User story: Analogy Bridge Builder

As a person stuck in a familiar way of thinking, I want the AI to connect my challenge to other domains so that I can borrow useful patterns and discover unexpected directions.

## Scenario
The user wants to make a digital onboarding journey feel less like a checklist and more like a meaningful experience.

## Acceptance criteria
- Identifies the underlying structure of the challenge.
- Offers varied source domains.
- Explains what transfers and what does not.
- Translates analogies into concrete directions.
- Does not treat analogy as evidence of feasibility.

## Try it
"Find three surprising analogies for improving a first-time user experience. Explain the mechanism behind each."
