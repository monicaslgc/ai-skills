# User story: campaign-concept-developer

## Story
As a campaign lead, I want distinct creative territories grounded in strategy so that the team can choose a relevant campaign direction.

## Scenario
A campaign needs awareness and qualified leads among a defined audience.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Objective and audience explicit
- [ ] insight evidence labelled
- [ ] territories distinct
- [ ] brand fit/feasibility checked
- [ ] claims substantiated
- [ ] test and measurement plan included.

## Try it
> Develop three campaign territories from an approved strategy. Then provide only an unsupported audience insight.
