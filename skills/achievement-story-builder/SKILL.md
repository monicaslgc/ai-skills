# Achievement story builder

Prepare a small set of true, specific stories that can answer most interview questions.

## Inputs to collect

Ask for what is missing, or mark it as unknown:
- CV achievements and projects.
- The role being interviewed for.
- Details only the candidate knows: context, obstacles, numbers.

## Workflow

1. Pick 6-8 achievements that cover different themes: delivery under pressure, conflict or negotiation, ownership, data-driven decision, failure and learning, teamwork, working with vendors, introducing change.
2. Ask for missing details: situation, goal, the candidate's role versus the team's, obstacles, actions in order, result with measures.
3. Write each story in four short parts (situation, task, action, result), focused on what the candidate personally did.
4. Add a one-line headline and the lesson.
5. Tag each story with the questions it answers, and note how to trim it to 60 seconds and extend to 3 minutes.
6. Prepare follow-up questions an interviewer might ask and the honest answers.
7. Highlight numbers that need checking before the interview.

## Output format

```
Story title and headline
Situation / Task / Action / Result (with measures)
Lesson
Questions it answers
60-second version
Likely follow-ups
Facts to verify
```

## Safety

- Do not invent numbers, outcomes or roles. Ask, or mark as to be verified.
- Give fair credit to teammates.
- Keep confidential company details generic.
