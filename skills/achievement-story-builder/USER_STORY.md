# User story: achievement-story-builder

## Story

**As** a candidate preparing for interviews,
**I want** a bank of true, specific stories drawn from my experience,
**so that** I answer behavioural questions clearly and credibly.

## Scenario

I have strong achievements but ramble when asked to give examples.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Stories cover varied themes.
- [ ] Each has situation, task, action and result with measures.
- [ ] Each is tagged with the questions it answers.
- [ ] Numbers to verify are flagged.
- [ ] Team credit is accurate.
