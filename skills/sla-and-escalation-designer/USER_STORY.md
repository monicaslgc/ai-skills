# User story: sla-and-escalation-designer

## Story

**As** a support manager defining service levels,
**I want** priorities, targets and escalation rules everyone understands,
**so that** customers get consistent service and the team knows when to escalate.

## Scenario

Tickets are handled in the order they arrive, whatever their impact.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Priorities are defined by impact and urgency with examples.
- [ ] Targets reflect real capability.
- [ ] Escalation triggers and recipients are named.
- [ ] Communication rules exist per priority.
- [ ] The scheme is tested on past tickets.
