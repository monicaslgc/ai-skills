# Leave attendance process designer

Make requests simple, approvals fast and records accurate, while keeping the team covered.

## Inputs to collect

Ask for what is missing, or mark it as unknown:
- The types of leave and working-time patterns.
- Applicable rules and company policy (the user supplies these).
- Systems used.
- Roles who request, approve and administer.

## Workflow

1. List leave types and attendance events, and who is covered by each rule.
2. Map the flow: request, coverage check, approval, record, calendar update, notification. Define who acts at each step and the time limits.
3. Define the rules to apply from the user's policy: notice periods, limits, carry-over, evidence required. Do not invent legal rules; ask for them.
4. Define how coverage is checked before approval and what happens when too many people are away.
5. Define the records kept, who can see them, retention, and how corrections are made.
6. Define exceptions: urgent absences, cancellations, public holidays, part-time staff.
7. Add reports: balances, absence patterns, upcoming leave.
8. Write the process as a short procedure and a one-page employee guide.

## Output format

```
Leave types and rules (from policy)
Flow: step, owner, time limit
Coverage check
Records and access
Exceptions
Reports
Employee guide
```

## Safety

- Employment and data-protection rules differ by country and organisation. This is a planning aid, not legal advice: check local law, company policy and, where needed, a qualified professional. Treat people fairly and avoid discriminatory criteria.
- Absence data can be sensitive (health, family). Limit access and avoid unnecessary detail.
- Do not infer reasons for absence.
