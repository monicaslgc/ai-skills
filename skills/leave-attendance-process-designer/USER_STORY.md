# User story: leave-attendance-process-designer

## Story

**As** an HR or team lead who manages absences,
**I want** a clear process for requesting, approving and recording leave,
**so that** people know how it works and the team stays covered.

## Scenario

Requests arrive by email and chat, and balances are out of date.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The flow has owners and time limits.
- [ ] Rules come from the supplied policy, not invented.
- [ ] Coverage is checked before approval.
- [ ] Records have access limits and correction rules.
- [ ] A one-page employee guide is produced.
