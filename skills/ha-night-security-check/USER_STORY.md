# User story: ha-night-security-check

## Story

**As** a homeowner going to bed or leaving,
**I want** a fast check of doors, locks, windows and lights,
**so that** I know what needs attention before I go.

## Scenario

I am about to leave for the weekend and cannot remember closing everything.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Locks, doors, windows, covers and alarm are reported.
- [ ] Unavailable sensors are not treated as safe.
- [ ] Actions are offered one by one with confirmation.
- [ ] Doors are never unlocked and alarms never disarmed.
- [ ] Unmonitored areas are mentioned.
