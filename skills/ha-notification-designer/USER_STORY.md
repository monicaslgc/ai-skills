# User story: ha-notification-designer

## Story

**As** a Home Assistant owner who ignores notifications,
**I want** a notification scheme with the right alerts at the right time,
**so that** important alerts get attention and noise stops.

## Scenario

My phone buzzes dozens of times a day and I started ignoring it.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Events are classified as urgent, important or information.
- [ ] Recipients, channels and quiet hours are defined.
- [ ] Noise is reduced by delay, grouping and digests.
- [ ] Messages say what, where and what to do.
- [ ] A test is sent only after confirmation.
