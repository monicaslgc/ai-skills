# User story: meeting-minutes-to-actions

## Story

**As** someone who attends many meetings and owns the follow-up,
**I want** notes turned into decisions and actions with owners and dates,
**so that** nothing agreed in the room gets lost afterwards.

## Scenario

I have a rough transcript of a one-hour planning meeting and need to send minutes today.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Decisions and actions are separated.
- [ ] Every action has a verb, an owner and a date, or is marked as missing.
- [ ] Open questions list who should answer.
- [ ] Nothing in the minutes is absent from the source.
- [ ] The summary is three lines or fewer.
