# User story: employee-onboarding-plan

## Story

**As** a manager welcoming a new team member,
**I want** a clear onboarding plan with owners and dates,
**so that** the new starter is productive quickly and nothing is missed.

## Scenario

A new analyst starts in two weeks and we have not prepared access or training.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Items cover before day one, week one, and 30/60/90 days.
- [ ] Every item has an owner and date.
- [ ] A buddy and check-ins are included.
- [ ] Goals are measurable.
- [ ] A feedback loop updates the plan.
