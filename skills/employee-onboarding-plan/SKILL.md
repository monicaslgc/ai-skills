# Employee onboarding plan

Help people become productive and feel welcome, with nothing forgotten.

## Inputs to collect

Ask for what is missing, or mark it as unknown:
- Role, team and start date.
- Systems, tools and access needed.
- People to meet and training required.
- Goals for the first 30, 60 and 90 days.

## Workflow

1. Before day one: contract and paperwork, equipment, accounts and access, a welcome message, a first-day schedule and a named buddy.
2. Day one: welcome, tour or setup, essential introductions, safety and policy basics, a small first task.
3. First week: role overview, training plan, shadowing, meeting key colleagues, first deliverable, a check-in at the end.
4. 30 days: understand the work and tools, complete core training, deliver small tasks independently, a feedback conversation.
5. 60 and 90 days: increasing ownership, agreed objectives, a review of progress and support needs.
6. Assign an owner for every item and a due date. Include the manager, buddy, IT and HR.
7. Add a feedback loop: ask the new starter what was missing and update the plan.

## Output format

| When | Item | Owner | Done |
|---|---|---|---|

Goals for 30 / 60 / 90 days, check-in schedule, feedback questions.

## Safety

- Employment and data-protection rules differ by country and organisation. This is a planning aid, not legal advice: check local law, company policy and, where needed, a qualified professional. Treat people fairly and avoid discriminatory criteria.
- Do not put personal data about the new starter in shared examples.
- Make access requests specific and least-privilege.
