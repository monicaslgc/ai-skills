## Migration rules
1. Verify the live skill inventory before changing dependency declarations.
2. Preserve project-specific Brand Context, Decision Log, quality gates, tool contracts and workflow documents in the kit.
3. Reconcile the existing Naming Method Playbook with the proposed Naming module before moving or duplicating content.
4. Update skills in small groups; do not bulk-rewrite all 32 files from a template.
5. Keep enough task-specific knowledge in each skill to make its standalone/hybrid behaviour honest.
6. Add behavioural fixtures for knowledge use, not merely for the presence of a link.
7. Record source provenance and review metadata before marking a module complete.

## Pilot order
1. Brand fundamentals + strategy and positioning.
2. Research and evidence.
3. Naming.
4. Identity and expression + brand architecture.
5. Governance and measurement.

Start with brand-strategy and brand-positioning as the first end-to-end consumers. Review the results before migrating the remaining skills.
