## Evidence and use notes

- Keller's CBBE and Aaker's brand-equity assets are related but not interchangeable.
- The positioning workflow in STRATEGY_AND_POSITIONING.md is a repository synthesis, informed by sources above and not presented as any author's original method.
- Practitioner methods structure work; claims about customer behaviour and market outcomes still require project-specific evidence.
- For legal, regulatory or standards compliance, verify current authoritative sources and jurisdiction. This knowledge base is not legal advice.
